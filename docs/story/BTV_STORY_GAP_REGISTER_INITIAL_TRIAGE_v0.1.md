# BTV STORY GAP REGISTER — INITIAL TRIAGE v0.1

**Status:** WORKING ANALYSIS / PROPOSED BACKLOG  
**Authority:** Below creator decisions and approved BTV source-of-truth documents.  
**Purpose:** Identify narrative work that can proceed safely and decisions that must remain open.

## 1. Important distinction

A story gap is not necessarily a plot hole. BTV deliberately uses mystery and incomplete information. This register distinguishes:
- **Intentional mystery:** retain until the story is ready to answer it.
- **Writing gap:** connective material is needed, but can be drafted without changing canon.
- **Decision gap:** creator approval is required before the story can commit.
- **Continuity risk:** sources or planned scenes may contradict each other.
- **Implementation dependency:** narrative design needs a gameplay/data system before it can be completed.

## 3. P0 writing order

1. Opening experience → Sunday/community context → Covenant handoff.
2. Scout Report presentation and player understanding.
3. Travel and Ridgeview arrival.
4. Ridgeview clue/revelation sequence.
5. Character knowledge and dialogue trigger matrix.
6. Five-approach outcome matrix.
7. Final report and local-resolution variants.
8. Post-mission Covenant debrief and transition hook, without exposing an unapproved larger mystery.

## 4. Deliberate mysteries — do not close in this pass

- The repairman's fate.
- The ultimate origin and metaphysics of the Veil.
- The identity, origin and full hierarchy of the pressure/group around Ridgeview.
- Any Ridgeview connection to the Concordance unless a reviewed evidence chain establishes it.
- The final status of the earlier dual-Chosen concept.
- Unapproved enemy origins, boss identities and campaign-ending revelations.

## 5. Required story-gap audit output

For each gap, the Story Development Agent must provide:
- source documents reviewed;
- exact canon statements relevant to the gap;
- what is missing and why it matters;
- whether the gap is intentional or accidental;
- dependencies and potential contradictions;
- 2–3 options where creator approval is required;
- a recommended next step;
- acceptance checks.

## 6. Scope and limitations

This is an initial triage derived from the currently inspected Story & Lore Bible and Ridgeview narrative pack. It is not a completed full-project audit. The remaining Master Canon, mission progression bible, character references, and all relevant source documents must be inspected before the register is treated as comprehensive.

No new canon, character, mission ending, Veil rule, enemy identity or implementation is approved by this register.
