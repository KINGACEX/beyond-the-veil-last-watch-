# BTV VERTICAL SLICE — OPENING-TO-RIDGEVIEW NARRATIVE FLOW MAP v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSED STORY STRUCTURE BUILT FROM APPROVED SLICE MATERIAL  
**Scope:** Logos Rhema Sunday → Covenant Scout Report → travel → Ridgeview investigation → player response → final Covenant report.  
**Authority:** Subordinate to BTV Master Canon v1.0 and Vertical Slice Specification v1.0. This document adds connective structure only; it does not promote new story facts to canon.

## 1. Narrative purpose

The opening should establish the ordinary human world before introducing the field case. The player first experiences a community that exists for its own reasons, then receives an uncertain operational report, then tests that report against reality.

The emotional and investigative progression is:

**Belonging and ordinary life → quiet interruption after worship → responsibility and uncertainty → observation → human testimony → evidence-based judgment → proportionate action → accountable reporting.**

The opening must not make church attendance a quest prerequisite, reward, stat system or timer challenge. It must not imply that Asher's field work is more spiritually valuable than ordinary worship, fellowship or service.

## 2. Sequence map

### Sequence A — Arrival and community life at Logos Rhema

**Purpose:** Establish the chapel as a living community, not a mission lobby.

**Established material:**
- Asher may arrive early and move freely.
- He may talk, help, sit, step outside quietly or skip a segment.
- There is no timer, forced objective marker or attendance reward.
- Ember accompanies Asher to the grounds, waits at the designated location under Hold and does not disrupt worship or Communion.
- The congregation is civilian-led; Covenant personnel do not dominate the space.

**Story beat:**
The player enters a community already in progress. Ordinary interactions are available, but no conversation is required to unlock the main story.

**Optional visitor beat:**
In Phase 1 or Phase 10, the visitor mentions one relative who went to work in a remote area and has missed check-ins. She does not name Ridgeview, explain the case or possess Covenant secrets.

**Do not add:**
- A named visitor or relative unless separately approved.
- A forced conversation or an explicit claim that the relative is the repairman.
- A supernatural clue in the church.
- A Covenant briefing during worship.

### Sequence B — Worship, teaching and Communion

**Purpose:** Give the community experience its own integrity and pace.

**Established material:**
- The service has a flexible structure and may include arrival/fellowship, welcome, praise and worship, prayer, Scripture reading, KITFIT, offering, sermon, response/reflection, Communion, announcements, closing prayer, fellowship and service.
- Philippians 4:13 (NIV) appears in Phase 3 in its context of contentment in every circumstance, not as a declaration mechanic.
- Offering is voluntary and has no reward, penalty, leaderboard, pressure or public amount.
- First Sunday Communion uses the Communion Set only in the CommunionContextZone.
- Deacon Amos Whitaker guides Communion in few words; the sequence is Prepare, Receive, Reflect, Pray, Continue.
- No gameplay reward is attached to attending, giving, praying or receiving Communion.

**Story beat:**
The player is allowed to remain in the service or leave quietly. The game does not frame the worship sequence as an obstacle to the “real” mission.

**Continuity check:**
No Covenant alert is surfaced until the service has ended, including closing prayer. The congregation does not see or learn of the alert.

### Sequence C — The quiet Covenant alert

**Purpose:** Shift from community life to field responsibility without turning the service into an interruption.

**Established material:**
- The alert is concerning, not an emergency.
- The Covenant Link queues it and delivers a quiet vibration only Asher notices after the ServiceDirector finishes.
- Asher chooses when to open it.
- No HUD marker appears before he opens it.
- Opening it grants nothing.

**Story beat:**
The player decides when to turn attention to the report. Lingering remains a valid choice.

**Open connective detail:**
The exact UI presentation, sender label, duty-officer wording and whether the alert is opened immediately or after additional fellowship are implementation/writing details. They must preserve the Scout as the intelligence source.

### Sequence D — Reading the Scout Report

**Purpose:** Give the player a credible but incomplete reason to investigate.

**Established material:**
- The Scout who gathered the information is the report's source.
- A duty officer may receive or forward it but does not replace the Scout as source.
- The report uses Observed, Reported, Inferred and Unconfirmed.
- It does not identify or hint at the Devourers.
- It includes alternative ordinary explanations and admits the Scout did not enter the outpost and cannot confirm anyone is in danger.
- The trader's claim that the outpost has been shut for weeks is second-hand and can be disproved on site.
- The visitor's relative connection remains unverified unless the player later finds supporting evidence.

**Player understanding at the end of the report:**
- Something about the approach and communications is unusual.
- A delivery was missed and travellers are overdue, but important details are uncertain.
- The Scout has not established that people are in immediate danger.
- The player is being asked to assess the situation, not to confirm a prewritten theory.

**Required report discipline:**
The report must distinguish what the Scout personally observed, what someone told the Scout, what the Scout inferred and what remains unknown. Do not add a hidden enemy identity or a definitive explanation.

### Sequence E — Decision to depart and field preparation

**Purpose:** Let the player accept the responsibility in a deliberate way.

**Established material:**
- The report carries a non-binding recommendation.
- The mission is concerning, not an emergency.
- The Covenant has a field vehicle and Asher travels with Ember.
- Asher is a Watcher; his core role is observation, investigation, evidence assessment and discernment.

**Proposed connective structure:**
- The player chooses to open the report and proceed to the field assignment.
- A brief transition communicates the practical objective: compare the report with conditions at Ridgeview and report what can actually be established.
- Avoid inventing a named commander, new rank, special prophecy, urgent countdown or new Covenant branch.

**Open questions:**
- Exact authorising role and wording for the assignment.
- Whether any additional team member accompanies Asher in this slice.
- What equipment is issued or already carried.
These should be resolved from existing Covenant and equipment references before final dialogue is written.

### Sequence F — The journey and screened pull-off

**Purpose:** Turn the report into a physical place and prepare the player to observe.

**Established material:**
- A practical Covenant field vehicle takes Asher and Ember to an access point near Ridgeview.
- Asher continues on foot.
- The journey is not merely a forced loading-screen fast travel.
- The guided route allows a free camera, ambient radio contact and a real stop at a screened pull-off.
- Arrival is late afternoon moving into evening.
- Covenant Link weakens past the ridge.

**Story beat:**
The player transitions from receiving second-hand information to making first-hand observations. The route should support atmosphere and orientation, not introduce an unrelated attack or lore dump.

**Open questions:**
- Exact radio speaker identity and dialogue.
- Whether the vehicle driver is named or remains an unnamed operational role.
- Exact pull-off interaction and what the player can inspect.
Do not invent identities or new story revelations to fill these spaces.

### Sequence G — Ridgeview approach and first contradiction

**Purpose:** Teach the player that a report is a starting point, not unquestionable truth.

**Established material:**
- The approach is unusually quiet; missing wildlife and muffled sound indicate a Disturbed state.
- The player can inspect the watched road, abandoned belongings, parked work vehicle/toolkit and beginning of the repairman's trail.
- The settlement is occupied, contradicting the trader's claim that it had been shut for weeks.
- Residents are wary but may answer if Asher listens first.
- The area becomes Influenced toward the settlement centre and camp: radio/devices become unreliable and the quiet deepens.
- The Veil's cause and ultimate nature remain unexplained.

**Player learning:**
- A second-hand report can contain an error.
- Direct observation can contradict a claim without proving every other claim false.
- A strange environment is not proof of a particular supernatural cause or human guilt.

### Sequence H — Wren, Pike and the outlying camp

**Purpose:** Put human testimony, responsibility and evidence in tension.

**Established material:**
- Wren is frightened and wants to leave. She signals that she wants a private conversation.
- Dorian Pike is the local leader, calm and persuasive, not a one-dimensional villain.
- Pike's true condition is uncertain until Asher gathers enough evidence.
- The outlying camp contains the hardest evidence about what became of the repairman, but not a complete explanation.
- The repairman remains missing; his fate is unresolved.
- Wren's testimony, Pike's statements and physical evidence must be recorded with their sources and limits.

**Story beat:**
The player is not told whom to trust. They compare what each person says with what can be observed. Listening is useful but does not make testimony automatically true.

**Do not add:**
- A definitive repairman fate.
- An automatic confession or complete villain reveal.
- A forced rescue or forced confrontation.
- A confirmed link to the Concordance or an unapproved wider conspiracy.
- An enemy name that the slice is not allowed to reveal.

### Sequence I — Evidence synthesis and meaningful response

**Purpose:** Convert observation into a defensible decision.

**Established material:**
- The Evidence Board does not reveal answers.
- Scout labels remain historical; Asher's local claim status is Confirmed, Contradicted or Still open.
- Player-created links remain Unverified until supported.
- Ember provides observations, warnings and possibilities, not answers.
- The five response options are Speak/Preach, Investigate, Rescue, Defend and Withdraw.
- Defend is available only when an authored credible threat or immediate danger justifies it.
- Options can combine; Withdraw is valid at any point.
- No option produces a morality, faith or conversion score.

**Story beat:**
The player decides what to do with incomplete knowledge. The narrative evaluates what actually happened, not what the player intended or what a menu option was called.

### Sequence J — Final report and local resolution

**Purpose:** End the slice with accountability and a meaningful local result, not a premature explanation of the whole game.

**Established material:**
- Asher files his own report.
- The report separates observed, reported, inferred and unconfirmed information.
- The system prevents claims from exceeding the evidence.
- The duty officer may ask hard questions and assign follow-up based on actual outcome flags.
- The Covenant is organised but not infallible and may disagree with Asher.
- The repairman remains unresolved in this slice.
- No promotion, rank change or reward is granted for the report.
- The player may return to Logos Rhema or continue.

**Story beat:**
The local case reaches a proportionate operational stopping point. The Covenant has enough information to decide what follow-up is justified, but not enough to claim that every mystery has been solved.

## 3. Required continuity invariants

1. Church activity is not a quest menu, timer or reward loop.
2. The alert never interrupts worship or Communion.
3. The Scout remains the source of the intelligence.
4. The report is uncertain and does not identify the hidden enemy.
5. The trader's error is disproved by direct observation, not by an unrelated exposition dump.
6. Asher's observations, testimony and inferences remain distinguishable.
7. Ember is not an oracle.
8. Veil influence is not a universal explanation for human choices.
9. Wren's consent and privacy are respected.
10. Pike's culpability is not assumed before evidence.
11. The repairman's fate remains unresolved.
12. The five approaches are real options with persisted outcomes.
13. Withdraw is never treated as an automatic failure.
14. The final report reflects the actual route and evidence.
15. No spiritual scoring, transactional prayer, forced conversion or Communion reward is introduced.
