# BTV Visual Production Quality Standard v1.0

**Status:** PROPOSED PRODUCTION STANDARD — creator review required  
**Applies to:** Concept art, key art, storyboards, in-game scenes, trailers, cutscenes, environments, characters, animation and UI.

## 1. Visual objectives

BTV visuals should be cinematic, readable, emotionally credible, grounded in the setting and recognisably consistent across scenes. The visual language should support an original Christian action-adventure survival RPG: serious, mysterious and hopeful, with restrained use of charcoal, white and muted gold where appropriate. Do not force every environment into one palette.

## 2. Pre-production gate

Before generation or implementation, every scene needs:
- scene ID, narrative purpose and player-facing context;
- approved story beat and canon dependencies;
- location/time/weather and environmental conditions;
- character/prop/creature references;
- shot list, camera framing and movement;
- lighting, atmosphere, colour and composition intent;
- action/blocking and continuity between shots;
- aspect ratio, resolution, target platform and asset budget;
- prohibited elements and unresolved design questions;
- approval and credit status.

## 3. Quality dimensions

Score each from 0–4: 0 absent/failed, 1 weak, 2 acceptable with revisions, 3 production-ready, 4 exceptional and consistent.

| Dimension | Review question |
|---|---|
| Canon fidelity | Does it preserve approved identities, designs, events and lore? |
| Composition | Is the subject clear, balanced and intentionally framed? |
| Cinematography | Do angle, lens/framing and movement serve the beat? |
| Lighting | Is light motivated, legible and consistent across cuts? |
| Environment | Does the location feel coherent, inhabited and specific? |
| Character continuity | Are silhouette, clothing, proportions and details stable? |
| Motion | Are movement, timing, transitions and physics coherent? |
| Materials/detail | Are textures, scale, surfaces and props credible? |
| Emotional clarity | Does the image communicate the intended feeling without misleading? |
| Technical readiness | Does it meet resolution, format, performance and Android constraints? |
| Accessibility | Is important information readable; are contrast and motion appropriate? |
| Provenance | Are source assets, permissions and generation/creation methods recorded? |

**Suggested gate:** no zero in any dimension; at least 3 in canon fidelity, composition, continuity and technical readiness before final approval. Creator may set a different threshold for a specific deliverable.

## 4. Continuity controls

Maintain reference sheets and asset IDs for approved characters, clothing, props, architecture, locations, colour/material systems and recurring visual motifs. Track camera direction, screen geography, time of day, weather, damage, carried objects and character state across adjacent shots.

Never use an unapproved generated image as the definitive reference for a locked character. Never infer Asher's appearance or Ember's species from anonymous placeholder imagery.

## 5. Scene review gates

- **G0 Brief:** story and canon dependencies checked.
- **G1 Design:** storyboard and references reviewed.
- **G2 Asset:** provenance, consistency and technical suitability checked.
- **G3 Assembly:** scene timing, camera, lighting, animation and transitions reviewed.
- **G4 QA:** defects logged and corrected; Android performance considered.
- **G5 Creator review:** final creative approval before canon adoption or publication.

## 6. Trailer and marketing integrity

Clearly distinguish concept art, illustrative stills, pre-rendered cinematic material and actual gameplay capture. Do not advertise a feature, character design or gameplay system as implemented unless verified in the build. No channel upload, public release or metadata change without explicit approval.

## 7. Defect severity

- **Blocker:** canon violation, misleading representation, missing essential scene, unusable output or serious technical failure.
- **Major:** obvious continuity, composition, motion, readability or asset-quality defect.
- **Minor:** polish issue that does not undermine comprehension.
- **Note:** optional improvement or subjective alternative.

Each defect must identify scene/asset, evidence, impact, proposed correction and owner.

## 8. Asset provenance

Record asset ID, creator/tool, creation date, source prompt/brief, reference assets, licence/permission basis, resolution/format, revisions and approval state. Do not use copyrighted or third-party assets without a suitable permission/licence basis. Do not commit API keys or account credentials.

## 9. Acceptance report

For each deliverable report scores, blockers, major/minor defects, canon status, technical test status, provenance completeness, unresolved decisions and creator approval state. A visual review is not a substitute for in-engine testing.
