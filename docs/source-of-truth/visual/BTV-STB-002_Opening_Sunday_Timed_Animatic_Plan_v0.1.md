**Total target runtime:** 90 seconds.

## 2. Editorial rules

- No Covenant alert before the service has ended.
- The alert is not a supernatural interruption to worship; it is a private, quiet cue.
- No visible HUD marker until Asher chooses to open the alert.
- The congregation does not know Asher is Covenant.
- Ember remains at his designated Hold location and does not interrupt worship or Communion.
- Communion has no buff, XP, healing, inventory item, or reward presentation.
- Offering is voluntary; never show giving as a score, ranking, or pressure mechanism.
- Do not invent Scripture quotations, lyrics, sermon lines, KITFIT expansion, or character dialogue.
- Do not use dramatic camera movement to turn ordinary worship into spectacle.

## 3. Production method

1. Use still frames or greybox camera positions first.
2. Build a rough animatic from the 12 approved-order frames.
3. Add only temporary timing cards and non-copyrighted scratch ambience.
4. Review service order and Covenant alert timing against the vertical slice.
5. Replace placeholder shots with approved environment and character assets.
6. Add original dialogue/music only after separate script and audio review.

## 4. Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Runtime lands between 85 and 95 seconds.
- [ ] All service phases remain in approved order.
- [ ] Covenant alert appears only after service ends.
- [ ] No unapproved Asher or Ember appearance is introduced.
- [ ] No unapproved Scripture text, lyrics, or dialogue appears.
- [ ] Communion and offering remain non-transactional.
- [ ] Shot rhythm feels natural and does not overdramatize church life.
- [ ] Creator review completed.
