## 1. Locked identity and role

- Name: Asher Vance.
- Age: 16–19; presented as 18 in the controlling vertical slice.
- Role: sole playable protagonist and sole Chosen in the current main-game direction.
- Covenant status: Agent/Specialist, Watcher branch.
- Human, never divine.
- Trained field operative who can defend himself and protect civilians.
- The Chosen does not replace Christ, God, or the Church.
- His progression is about responsibility, service, trust, leadership, judgment, and consequences—not kill/XP grinding.

The current main-game direction keeps Asher as the sole playable Chosen. The older selectable male/female Chosen structure is retired; it must not be reintroduced as a second playable protagonist. A separate important non-playable Chosen remains possible only within the approved story direction.

## 2. Visual identity currently UNKNOWN

## 4. Visual direction that may be explored without locking appearance

PROPOSED:
- grounded cinematic realism consistent with Foundation #12 v1.1;
- contemporary, practical field-operator clothing;
- restrained Covenant identity, not overt public display;
- credible materials and functional equipment;
- clear visual distinction between ordinary church attendance and Covenant field duty;
- mobile-readable silhouette and facial performance.

These are production directions, not permission to decide Asher's face or body.

## 5. Forbidden visual drift

- Do not age Asher beyond the 16–19 range or present him as older than the slice's age-18 presentation.
- Do not portray him as divine, superhuman, spiritually infallible, or automatically superior.
- Do not turn him into a generic medieval knight, fantasy paladin, superhero, or futuristic soldier.
- Do not expose Covenant identity to the congregation during Logos Rhema scenes.
- Do not invent a signature weapon, combat loadout, or supernatural power from a reference mockup.
- Do not add a second playable Chosen.

## 6. Required creator decision before final character art

A final model sheet requires selection or replacement of the authoritative visual identity. The exact face, hair, skin tone, build, and clothing must be confirmed in a single coherent direction. This brief deliberately does not choose between the inconsistent references.
