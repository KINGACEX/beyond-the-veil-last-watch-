## 1. Source authority

The controlling Vertical Slice Specification v1.0 sets the service phases in this order:
1. Arrival and Fellowship
2. Welcome
3. Praise and Worship
4. Prayer
5. Scripture Reading
6. KITFIT
7. Offering
8. Sermon
9. Response and Reflection
10. Communion
11. Announcements
12. Closing Prayer
13. Fellowship and Service

The Covenant alert is available only after the service ends. It does not interrupt worship, prayer, Scripture, KITFIT, offering, sermon, Communion, or closing prayer. Asher may choose when to open it. It is a quiet vibration only he notices, and no HUD marker appears until he opens it. The storyboard's small rectangular cue is an abstract placeholder for the vibration/alert, not a locked smartphone or device design.

## 2. Twelve-frame board coverage

| Frame | Phase / beat | Visual intent |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Arrival and Fellowship | Establish a calm, welcoming space and the first arrivals |
| 02 | Welcome | Show community hospitality and belonging |
| 03 | Praise and Worship | Respectful communal worship without invented lyrics or spectacle |
| 04 | Prayer | Quiet reflection, no mechanical effect |
| 05 | Scripture Reading | Scripture is present without generating unapproved on-screen verse text |
| 06 | KITFIT | A short Scripture-based teaching about giving; acronym expansion remains UNKNOWN |
| 07 | Offering | Voluntary giving, with no visible amounts, pressure, or comparison |
| 08 | Sermon | Teaching within the community's life |
| 09 | Response and Reflection | Quiet reflection; no forced prompt or morality score |
| 10 | Communion | Bread and cup in context; no buff, XP, healing, or transaction |
| 11 | Announcements and Closing Prayer | Practical notices and a closing moment |
| 12 | Fellowship and post-service alert | Fellowship continues; only after service ends does Asher notice the queued alert |

The board combines Announcements and Closing Prayer in one frame for visual economy. The approved service order remains unchanged.

## 3. Character and world continuity

- The storyboard uses generic silhouettes only. It does not establish Asher's face, skin tone, hair, body proportions, clothing, or equipment.
- The congregation does not know Asher is Covenant. Do not show a Covenant uniform or visible affiliation in church scenes.
- Ember waits at the designated spot under Hold with a clear line to Asher. He does not enter or disrupt worship or Communion.
- Ember's exact appearance remains open; no fox or wolf-like identity is established by this board.
- The church remains a living community space, not a Covenant facility or gameplay reward station.

## 4. Audio and dialogue constraints

- No dialogue has been written or approved by this storyboard.
- No song lyrics are shown or assumed.
- No Scripture passage is displayed in full.
- Any future Scripture quotation must be verified in context and checked for translation/rights.
- Ambient sound, music, and spoken content remain open.
