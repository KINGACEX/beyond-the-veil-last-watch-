## 1. Source-grounded slice facts

The controlling Vertical Slice Specification v1.0 establishes:
- Asher approaches Ridgeview late afternoon moving into evening.
- The approach is Disturbed: missing wildlife, muffled sound, and Ember more alert.
- The settlement and outlying camp are Influenced: unreliable radio/devices, uneasy residents, and quiet that deepens toward the camp.
- The settlement has residents; the outlying camp holds evidence relevant to the missing repairman.
- The Veil creates no monsters in this slice, does not excuse human choices, and remains unexplained.
- No children appear at Ridgeview.
- No on-screen remains, gore, or graphic description of injury, death, bodies, remains, or violence.
- The Devourers are not named or identified in dialogue, UI, classification, or Asher's report.
- Ordinary explanations remain plausible; the scene must not force one interpretation.

## 2. Three visual zones

### A. Approach
A practical path from the screened vehicle access point toward the settlement. Late-afternoon light transitions toward dusk. The atmosphere is quiet, with a subdued environment. Do not show a visible monster, portal, supernatural beam, or explanatory symbol.

### B. Settlement
A modest inhabited rural settlement. Adults may be present, wary and observant. Keep civilian life plausible; avoid turning every resident into an enemy or an exposition device. Do not depict children in this slice.

### C. Outlying work camp
A practical worksite with a modest shelter, equipment, and evidence-bearing details. Its visual storytelling should rely on ordinary objects, absence, and environmental clues rather than graphic imagery or explicit threat depiction.

## 3. Concept design choices (PROPOSED)

- Low-rise, practical buildings and work structures.
- Muted earth materials and weathered surfaces.
- A late-afternoon-to-dusk lighting progression.
- Restrained environmental contrast; no high-saturation supernatural VFX.
- Readable paths and spatial landmarks for third-person exploration.
- Sparse, carefully placed adult silhouettes rather than a dense crowd.
- No visible enemy entities.

All building forms, materials, exact terrain, vegetation, and settlement layout remain proposals pending comparison with approved source references and the Godot greybox.
