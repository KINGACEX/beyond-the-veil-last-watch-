## 1. Locked visual constraints

Foundation #11 establishes Ember as:
- a rare, original wild-dog/canid lineage;
- a natural creature of God's creation, not artificially or occult-created;
- athletic, agile, resilient, expressive, and believable;
- a companion and field partner, not a weapon or inventory item;
- perceptive but fallible, with limited Veil sensitivity;
- an animal who communicates through posture, movement, attention, distance, ears, eyes, breathing, vocalizations, rest, and proximity—not human dialogue.

The exact visual breed characteristics remain OPEN.

## 2. Three silhouette directions

The SVG contains three deliberately different, neutral concept directions:
- **A — Lean / Long-stride:** endurance-oriented proportions.
- **B — Balanced / Resilient:** moderate proportions and sturdy build.
- **C — Agile / High-awareness:** lighter proportions and an alert natural posture.

These are visual exploration options only. They do not establish Ember's final size, build, coat, markings, breed, or anatomy.

## 3. Prohibited directions

Do not turn Ember into:
- a fox by default;
- a standard wolf or a wolf-man;
- a fantasy monster;
- an angelic, demonic, winged, cybernetic, or artificially engineered creature;
- a human personality speaking through an animal body.

A previous Library file named `btv_fox_companion_v03_preview.png` depicts a stylized fox-like preview. It is not an approved BTV Ember reference and does not override Foundation #11.

## 4. Production decision still required

Before producing a final model sheet, the creator must select or revise the overall silhouette direction. After that, detailed reference work can explore head profile, muzzle, ears, torso proportions, limb length, paws, tail, coat, and markings.

No final 3D model, rig, animation, or gameplay asset is produced by this study.
