## Source locks

This sequence is grounded in the controlling Vertical Slice Specification v1.0:
- Logos Rhema is a fictional church, not a Covenant base.
- The church is a living community activity.
- The service phases are: Arrival and Fellowship, Welcome, Praise and Worship, Prayer, Scripture Reading, KITFIT, Offering, Sermon, Response and Reflection, Communion, Announcements, Closing Prayer, Fellowship and Service.
- Asher may arrive early, talk, help, sit, step outside quietly, or skip a segment. There is no timer or forced objective.
- Ember waits at a designated spot under Hold; he does not disrupt worship or Communion.
- The Covenant alert is delivered only after the service ends and does not interrupt any service phase.
- The congregation does not know Asher is Covenant.
- No service attendance reward, buff, XP, Aces, Credits, equipment, stats, or progression is granted.

## Board frame notes

| Frame | Purpose | Camera / staging | Continuity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Establish | Establish room geography and quiet before service | Wide, centered, slow push or locked-off | No urgency markers; no Covenant staging |
| 02 Fellowship | Show a living congregation | Medium-wide, lateral coverage | Civilians and worshippers dominate |
| 03 Worship | Communicate communal praise respectfully | Wide-to-medium coverage; restrained movement | No invented song lyrics or spectacle |
| 04 Scripture / KITFIT | Show teaching as part of service | Medium shot on speaker and open book | Do not invent Scripture text or KITFIT expansion |
| 05 Offering | Show voluntary giving | Detail shot of a simple collection moment | No amounts, leaderboard, pressure, or visible comparative wealth |
| 06 Communion | Quiet contextual moment | Restrained close details of bread/cup and congregation | No buff, reward, XP, healing, or supernatural transaction |

The SVG contains four drawn panels and a written continuity strip for the final two phases; it is a composition board, not a frame-accurate animatic. The remaining phases are specified in the shot plan and must be added before a full animatic.
