# BEYOND THE VEIL: THE LAST WATCH
## Canon Reconciliation & Story/Veil Decisions v1.1

**Purpose:** Resolve BTV canon conflicts and establish a coherent story/Veil direction.

**Scope:** This document addresses only (1) Canon Reconciliation and (3) Story and Veil Decisions. It does not specify the full core gameplay model, technical proof, vertical-slice completion plan, or expansion beyond the vertical slice.

**Authority:** This is a creator-delegated decision set prepared at the user's request to handle all audit items except #2, #4, #5, and #6. It should be treated as the current recommended reconciliation addendum. Existing explicit creator decisions and locked vertical-slice details remain protected. Any future direct creator correction supersedes this addendum.

## 1. Canon authority and status vocabulary

### 1.1 Authority order
1. The creator's latest explicit instruction or correction.
2. The BTV Master Canon, as reconciled by accepted creator decisions.
3. Specialized Bibles for their own domains (Story & Lore, Character, Covenant, Faith/Prayer/Armor, Church/Logos Rhema, Veil & Enemy, World/NPC, Mission/Progression/Economy, Ember, Visual/UI/HUD, Technical).
4. The Vertical Slice Specification for its explicitly locked slice details; it may not silently rewrite global canon.
5. Older handoffs, prompts, prototypes, mockups, and generated assets.

When two sources conflict, do not blend them into a third contradictory version. Record the conflict, follow the higher authority, and update the lower-authority source when convenient.

### 1.2 Standard status labels
- **CANON:** Established and protected unless the creator changes it.
- **APPROVED DESIGN:** Approved to build or use; implementation details may evolve.
- **CANON DIRECTION:** A firm creative direction, with some details still open.
- **RESERVED:** Intentionally deferred; do not implement or expand prematurely.
- **PROPOSED:** A suggestion awaiting explicit approval.
- **UNKNOWN / CREATOR DECISION REQUIRED:** Not established; do not invent as fact.
- **CONFLICT:** Contradicts a higher-authority decision and must not be followed.

Do not use “approved,” “locked,” or “canon” interchangeably without specifying which status applies.

## 2. Reconciled canon decisions

### 2.1 Protagonist and Chosen structure — CANON
- Asher Vance is the sole playable protagonist and the Chosen.
- Asher is presented as 18, within the established 16–19 range; he is human and never divine.
- The selectable male/female dual-Chosen concept is retired from active canon. The former second-Chosen concept is not an active alternate route.
- Asher begins as an Agent/Specialist in the Watcher branch. “Agent/Specialist” is one tier; Agent and Specialist describe function, not separate ranks.
- Advancement to Team Leader, Commander, and General comes through story-earned responsibility, judgment, service, and consequences—not XP grinding or spiritual superiority.

### 2.2 Logos Rhema Temple and real-world inspiration — CANON
- Harvest Chapel International (HCI) is real-world inspiration/reference only; it does not appear in the game under its real name.
- Logos Rhema Temple is the fictional in-game church. It must have original characters, dialogue, architecture, and presentation; real HCI pastors are not in-game characters.
- KITFIT is a Logos Rhema segment. The expansion of the acronym remains UNKNOWN and must not be guessed.
- The church is a living community space, not a Covenant base. Its members do not automatically know Asher's Covenant affiliation.
- Giving is voluntary and is not a purchase of faith, divine favour, status, or gameplay advantage. Communion remains contextual and is not a buff or stat system.

### 2.3 The Covenant — CANON
- Rank order: Recruit → Agent/Specialist → Team Leader → Commander → General.
- Branches: Guardians, Messengers, Watchers, Shepherds, Scouts, Keepers.
- The Covenant is a hidden service/protection organization, not a government agency and not a replacement for the Church.
- Rank represents responsibility and trust, never spiritual superiority or closeness to God.
- The Covenant oath remains: “By truth we stand. By faith we endure. By the Light we rise. Darkness will not prevail.”

### 2.4 Ember — CANON
- Ember is Asher's intelligent animal companion and field partner, not a weapon or an omniscient guide.
- Official commands: **Attack, Protect, Scout, Track, Retrieve, Hold, Regroup.** “Stay” is legacy/informal wording for Hold; Follow and Guard are not official commands.
- Established ability names: Veil Sight, Guardian Instinct, Veil Energy, Tracking Sense, Spirit Guidance. These names do not establish Ember's species, origin, or ultimate nature.
- Ember's species, exact origin, history, and full relationship to the Veil remain UNKNOWN unless separately resolved by a later creator decision.

### 2.5 Enemies and Ridgeview — CANON / SCOPE-SPECIFIC
- The main functional enemy classes are Destroyers, Persuaders, Deceivers, and Commanders/Bosses. These are roles/functions, not a complete taxonomy of species or metaphysical origins.
- Devourers are a rare, disturbing, choice-driven crisis group established for the Ridgeview storyline. Their portrayal must remain non-graphic and humane. In the vertical slice, the group is not named to the player; do not reveal the label in its dialogue, objectives, or UI.
- Human antagonists must have specific motives. Being outside the Covenant, being a civilian, or being a non-believer does not make a person evil.
- Some encounters may support investigation, dialogue, warning, preaching, de-escalation, rescue, defense, retreat, or combat. Conversion is never a percentage, guaranteed outcome, or reward condition.

### 2.6 Existing vertical-slice locks remain intact
- The slice begins with Asher's first Sunday Communion at Logos Rhema Temple, followed by a Covenant alert, Scout Report, evidence review, journey, Ridgeview investigation, response, and final report.
- Psalm 23 is used at Ridgeview in the established authored scene; it is not a spell, attack, stat buff, or universal solution.
- No choice in that slice awards XP or a morality score.
- Named slice characters and all locked slice boundaries remain as written in the current Vertical Slice Specification. This addendum does not redesign or expand that slice.

### 2.7 Currency and faith boundaries — CANON
- Aces are general-world currency; Covenant Credits are Covenant-focused currency.
- Faith, salvation, prayer, divine favour, spiritual worth, and the Holy Spirit are never purchasable, lootable, or represented by a universal morality meter.
- Prayer is meaningful but not a guaranteed transaction. The Holy Spirit is not a controllable combat ability, scanner, or certainty engine.

## 3. Story decisions

### 3.1 Narrative identity — CANON DIRECTION
BTV's main conflict is about **truth, trust, service, fear, manipulation, community protection, and redemption** during a growing pattern of Veil incidents. It is not simply a campaign to kill increasingly powerful monsters. The modern world remains lived-in; ordinary civilians and communities are the reason the Covenant acts.

### 3.2 Main antagonist — APPROVED STORY DIRECTION
The central human antagonist is **Marek Sloane**, the architect of a covert crisis-manipulation network provisionally named **the Concordance**.

- **Motive:** Sloane believes that uncontrolled truth and public uncertainty create chaos. He seeks to control what communities are allowed to know, claiming that managed information and enforced order are safer than people making informed choices.
- **Method:** The Concordance redirects reports, exploits institutional weaknesses, pressures witnesses, spreads convincing misinformation, and takes advantage of Veil incidents. It does not create the Veil and cannot command it at will.
- **Moral framing:** Sloane is responsible for his choices, but he is not a stand-in for all non-believers, all officials, all researchers, or all people who fear the unknown. Some people inside his network are opportunists; others are misled, coerced, or capable of withdrawing and making amends.
- **Threat identity:** He is a human antagonist, not the Antichrist, Satan, God, or a substitute for a final supernatural explanation. His power comes from access, influence, selective truth, and exploiting people's fear—not omniscience.
- **Status boundary:** The name Marek Sloane and the Concordance are the recommended story decisions in this delegated plan. Specific biographies, full faction membership, and named lieutenants are not to be expanded until they are needed for approved story work.

### 3.3 Main story progression — CANON DIRECTION
**Act I — The First Report:** The Logos Rhema Sunday and Ridgeview investigation introduce Asher's ordinary community life, the Covenant's hidden responsibilities, uncertain evidence, the Devourer crisis, and the principle that not every encounter has a simple combat answer.

**Act II — The Pattern:** Later missions reveal that separate reports share signs of coordinated misinformation and deliberate exploitation. Asher gathers corroborated evidence; the player learns that a correct-looking report may still be incomplete, and a frightened witness may be mistaken without being malicious.

**Act III — The Trust Crisis:** Concordance interference causes communities and institutions to distrust one another. Persuaders and Deceivers exploit real grievances, rumours, and Veil effects. Covenant teams disagree over risk and disclosure, creating leadership choices without turning the Covenant into a flawless institution.

**Act IV — The Last Watch:** A major, multi-location crisis threatens civilians and essential community links. Sloane tries to use the confusion to impose his preferred order and conceal the network's role. Asher's defining test is to protect people, preserve evidence, coordinate with the Covenant, and expose the manipulation rather than sacrifice civilians merely to reach the antagonist first.

**Resolution:** The Concordance's operation is broken and its evidence is exposed to appropriate authorities and communities. Sloane is held accountable, with the exact legal outcome depending on the final story presentation. People harmed by the network receive practical support; some can repent or cooperate, but reconciliation is not automatic. The Covenant earns greater trust through service and transparency about its own limits.

**Ending boundary:** The central human plot reaches a satisfying resolution, but the Veil itself is not destroyed or fully explained. The world is safer because people and communities respond more wisely—not because Asher gains total supernatural control.

### 3.4 The Seven Deadly Sins, Antichrist, and redemption — RESERVED
- The Seven Deadly Sins remain thematic lenses for temptation, harm, habits, and social consequences—not seven automatic boss monsters.
- Antichrist-related material is reserved. The first game's main antagonist is not identified as the Antichrist, and BTV must not assert speculative end-times claims as unquestionable biblical fact.
- Redemption is possible but never guaranteed, instant, consequence-free, or tied to a hidden conversion score.

## 4. Veil decisions

### 4.1 Core definition — CANON DIRECTION
The Veil is a **mysterious supernatural boundary-condition that can disrupt the consistency of perception, information, environments, and events**. It is not a conventional portal, a simple alternate dimension, a moral alignment meter, or a replacement for God, Christ, Scripture, prayer, or the Holy Spirit.

The story may present competing theories about its ultimate origin. BTV's first game does not confirm a final cosmic explanation. This is an intentional mystery, not permission for the Veil to behave inconsistently.

### 4.2 Operational laws — APPROVED DESIGN
1. **Coherence:** Each major Veil incident follows a discoverable pattern. It may be hard to interpret, but it does not change rules merely to force a plot twist.
2. **Locality:** Effects are concentrated around a situation, site, object, information chain, or linked set of conditions. A crisis may spread, but its spread must have clues and limits.
3. **Evidence:** Important incidents leave evidence across more than one channel where practical—environmental signs, witness accounts, records, recordings, behavior, or physical traces. Any single channel may be incomplete or deceptive.
4. **Limited reach:** The Veil does not grant automatic omniscience, unlimited reality rewriting, perfect possession, or unrestricted control over every person's thoughts.
5. **Agency:** Influence is not the same as total control. People retain agency, and exposure to the Veil does not determine a person's morality, salvation, or spiritual worth.
6. **Escalation:** Incidents can escalate, stabilize, recede, or change form. Escalation depends on story-grounded conditions and consequences, not merely on the player's level.
7. **Counterplay:** Observation, corroboration, preparation, protection, withdrawal, teamwork, and learning patterns can help. No universal detector or single action solves every incident.
8. **Human exploitation:** People can exploit Veil incidents without understanding their ultimate nature. Human schemes may intensify danger but do not explain every Veil event.
9. **Faith boundary:** Prayer and Scripture can shape a character's courage, discernment, reflection, and decisions. Neither functions as a password that automatically reveals or closes the Veil. The Holy Spirit is never reduced to a game-system sensor.
10. **Ember boundary:** Ember may sense clues or react to danger, but can be uncertain, distracted, mistaken, or limited by conditions. Ember never replaces investigation or the player's judgment.
11. **Civilian continuity:** Most of the world remains ordinary and inhabited. Veil incidents are meaningful because they disturb real places and lives, not because every street is a supernatural combat zone.
12. **Resolution:** A local incident may be contained, escaped, investigated, disrupted, or resolved by changing its conditions and protecting people. No local victory proves that the Veil has been globally defeated.

### 4.3 Six-stage incident scale — APPROVED DESIGN
These are incident states, not RPG levels, moral states, or guaranteed linear stages.

1. **Normal:** No active Veil effects are reliably observed.
2. **Disturbed:** Subtle anomalies, conflicting observations, or unreliable signals appear; evidence is ambiguous.
3. **Influenced:** A localized environment, information chain, or behavior pattern is measurably affected; danger may remain indirect.
4. **Veil-Touched:** A localized inconsistency becomes a tangible safety concern. Evidence exists, but its interpretation may still be disputed.
5. **Veil Breach:** A dangerous localized manifestation or environmental inconsistency is active. Protection, evacuation, investigation, and containment may be necessary.
6. **Critical / Endgame-Level:** Multiple linked incidents or a sustained breach threaten a wider community or region. This is a crisis classification, not proof of a final cosmic entity or unlimited Veil power.

Not every incident must pass through every state. An event may remain subtle, recede, or be interrupted before escalating. Reports must identify what is observed and what is inferred.

### 4.4 Enemy relationship to the Veil — CANON DIRECTION
- **Destroyers:** Direct-aggression threats. Their origins and species are not automatically established by their class name.
- **Persuaders:** Influence, manipulation, temptation, coercion, and exploitation. They cannot simply overwrite a person's agency at will.
- **Deceivers:** False perception, misinformation, ambiguity, misdirection, and ambush. Their deceptions leave patterns or costs that careful investigation can uncover.
- **Commanders/Bosses:** Organize or embody a specific crisis. A boss must express its narrative role and the incident's rules, not merely have more health.
- Enemy knowledge must have a plausible source and limits. The taxonomy is functional; it is not a universal hierarchy or proof of a single master entity.

### 4.5 Unknowns intentionally preserved — UNKNOWN / RESERVED
The following remain open because prematurely defining them would weaken the mystery or require broader creator review:
- The Veil's ultimate origin and complete metaphysics.
- Whether any single entity controls, created, or fully understands the Veil.
- Complete ancient history, named supernatural hierarchy, and ultimate cosmology.
- Ember's exact species, origin, and metaphysical relationship to the Veil.
- Detailed origins and full hierarchy of every enemy class.
- The exact final legal outcome for Marek Sloane and the Concordance.

These unknowns are bounded: writers must obey the operational laws above and may not use an unknown as an excuse to contradict established behavior.

## 6. Change-control rule

Before a new major character, enemy, Veil event, boss, or story arc is added, record:
- its status label;
- which established canon it depends on;
- what the player can know versus what remains uncertain;
- its effect on civilians and communities;
- its faith/theology boundary;
- whether it changes any locked vertical-slice content;
- and any contradiction that must be resolved first.

**Final direction:** BTV's mysteries should be intriguing because evidence is incomplete—not because the world has no rules. Its antagonist should be dangerous because he manipulates real human weaknesses—not because he can do anything. Its Christian identity should shape meaning, courage, service, and hope without turning God, prayer, Scripture, or the Holy Spirit into controllable game mechanics.
