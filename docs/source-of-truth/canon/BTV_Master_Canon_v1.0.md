# 1. CANON STATUS SYSTEM

Every meaningful BTV statement or design element must be understood through one of five statuses.

### CANON
Explicitly established and protected.

### PROPOSED
A serious idea under consideration, but not yet approved as permanent canon.

### ALTERNATIVE
A legitimate alternate direction that may be selected later.

### UNKNOWN
Not established. It must not be filled with invented lore merely to make the world appear complete.

Preferred wording:

> UNKNOWN — NOT ESTABLISHED IN CURRENT BTV CANON.

### CONFLICT
Two or more established sources appear incompatible. Do not silently choose a side. Identify the conflict and request/await a creator decision.

# 2. PROJECT IDENTITY

**Beyond the Veil: The Last Watch** is an original modern Christian action-adventure survival RPG.

BTV combines:
- action,
- survival,
- exploration,
- investigation,
- social interaction,
- rescue,
- leadership,
- spiritual conflict,
- community life,
- redemption,
- and a large interconnected/semi-open world.

The setting is modern rather than a direct retelling of biblical events.

Christianity is not cosmetic decoration. Faith, service, truth, redemption, prayer, spiritual warfare, perseverance, and moral decisions influence the world's philosophy and the player's experience.

BTV must remain an original work.

# 5. GOD, CHRIST, AND THE HOLY SPIRIT

## 5.1 God

**CANON**

God is the ultimate authority in BTV.

God is not a playable combat character.

The Covenant does not command God, control God, or replace God.

The game must avoid presenting divine action as a predictable mechanical vending machine.

## 5.2 Christ

**CANON**

Christ is above the Covenant and above the Chosen.

The Chosen is never a replacement for Christ, a divine being, or an equivalent spiritual authority.

BTV's Christian worldview must preserve this distinction.

## 5.3 Holy Spirit

**CANON**

The Holy Spirit may be represented through concepts such as:
- guidance,
- conviction,
- warnings,
- wisdom,
- discernment,
- spiritual awareness.

The Holy Spirit is not a player-controlled attack resource.

There is no canonical system in which a player spends a resource to activate "Holy Spirit powers."

# 6. PRAYER

**CANON**

Prayer is a meaningful part of BTV.

Prayer is not:
- a guaranteed transaction,
- a purchasable buff,

## 70. Creator-approved economy/progression and production-discipline addendum — 2026-10-11

**Status: APPROVED DESIGN.** This addendum records approved cross-project design principles. It does not replace established content canon, authorize changes to reserved collaborative areas, or claim code implementation or testing.

1. **Three reward categories:** ordinary money (Aces), Covenant resources (Covenant Credits and authorized supplies), and non-monetary outcomes remain distinct. Avoid unnecessary new currencies.
2. **Progression without spiritual scoring:** practical capability, Covenant responsibility, Ember growth, and contextual relationships remain distinct. None measures spiritual worth. Sacred Christian realities and practices are not purchasable, tradable, grindable, or commercialized rewards.
3. **World recovery:** appropriate rescue, investigation, restoration, and community outcomes may change future access, NPC reactions, available information, services, safety, and local conditions. Consequences should be understandable and proportionate.
4. **Lore-grounded Covenant logistics:** Keepers, Scouts, Watchers, and leadership ground supply, reports, equipment, and operational authorizations in the established organization and its responsibilities. Detailed mechanics remain open unless separately approved.
5. **Evidence-based gates:** documentation, implementation, integration, verification, and acceptance are separate states, each requiring evidence. A design document alone proves documentation only; do not claim code implementation or tests without evidence.
6. **One source of truth:** the Master Canon remains the primary content-canon authority; each existing specialized Bible is the domain reference for its system. Record a decision in its proper existing reference and cross-reference material changes rather than creating contradictory parallel specifications.
