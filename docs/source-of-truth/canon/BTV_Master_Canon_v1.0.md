# 1. CANON STATUS SYSTEM

Every meaningful BTV statement or design element must be understood through one of five statuses.

### CANON
Explicitly established and protected.

### PROPOSED
A serious idea under consideration, but not yet approved as permanent canon.

### ALTERNATIVE
A legitimate alternate direction that may be selected later.

### UNKNOWN
Not established. It must not be filled with invented lore merely to make the world appear complete.

Preferred wording:

> UNKNOWN — NOT ESTABLISHED IN CURRENT BTV CANON.

### CONFLICT
Two or more established sources appear incompatible. Do not silently choose a side. Identify the conflict and request/await a creator decision.

# 2. PROJECT IDENTITY

**Beyond the Veil: The Last Watch** is an original modern Christian action-adventure survival RPG.

BTV combines:
- action,
- survival,
- exploration,
- investigation,
- social interaction,
- rescue,
- leadership,
- spiritual conflict,
- community life,
- redemption,
- and a large interconnected/semi-open world.

The setting is modern rather than a direct retelling of biblical events.

Christianity is not cosmetic decoration. Faith, service, truth, redemption, prayer, spiritual warfare, perseverance, and moral decisions influence the world's philosophy and the player's experience.

BTV must remain an original work.

---

# 3. CORE CREATIVE PRINCIPLE

BTV should feel like a serious, premium adventure RPG whose Christian worldview is genuinely integrated into its world rather than pasted onto conventional gameplay.

The game should be capable of:
- intense action,

- meaningful conversations,
- community activity,
- investigation,
- danger,
- grief,
- hope,
- restoration,
- leadership,

# 5. GOD, CHRIST, AND THE HOLY SPIRIT

## 5.1 God

**CANON**

God is the ultimate authority in BTV.

God is not a playable combat character.

The Covenant does not command God, control God, or replace God.

The game must avoid presenting divine action as a predictable mechanical vending machine.

## 5.2 Christ

**CANON**

Christ is above the Covenant and above the Chosen.

The Chosen is never a replacement for Christ, a divine being, or an equivalent spiritual authority.

BTV's Christian worldview must preserve this distinction.

## 5.3 Holy Spirit

**CANON**

The Holy Spirit may be represented through concepts such as:
- guidance,
- conviction,
- warnings,
- wisdom,
- discernment,
- spiritual awareness.

The Holy Spirit is not a player-controlled attack resource.

There is no canonical system in which a player spends a resource to activate "Holy Spirit powers."

---

# 6. PRAYER

**CANON**

Prayer is a meaningful part of BTV.

Prayer is not:
- a guaranteed transaction,
- a purchasable buff,
- a combat spell,
- a mana system,
- or a "more prayer points = stronger God" mechanic.

Prayer may occur:
- during difficult situations,
- after missions,
- at meaningful locations,
- before decisions,
- during uncertainty,
- or during personal reflection.

Results are not guaranteed simply because the player prayed.

Divine timing and God's will remain conceptually distinct from game-stat optimization.

# 7. THE CHOSEN

**CANON**

The Chosen is an important human protagonist.

The Chosen:
- is not divine,
- does not replace Christ,
- is not God,
- is not spiritually superior merely because of rank,

The established protagonist name is:

Asher Vance's established age range is:

Leadership progression is based on:

- responsibility,
- decisions,
- trust,
- leadership,
- and demonstrated capability.

The established progression framework is:

**Field role → Team Leader → Commander → General**

The exact story timing and implementation remain subject to development decisions.

# 9. THE COVENANT

**CANON**

The Covenant is a hidden organization serving God and protecting people.

It is:
- not a government agency,
- not the entirety of Christianity,
- not a replacement for the Church,
- and not above Christ.

The Covenant operates in a world where ordinary people continue to live, work, worship, trade, travel, and form communities.

Its purpose is service and protection, not spiritual superiority.
