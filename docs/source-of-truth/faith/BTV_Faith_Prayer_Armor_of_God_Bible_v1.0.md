# BEYOND THE VEIL: THE LAST WATCH
## FAITH, PRAYER & ARMOR OF GOD BIBLE v1.0

**Document Type:** Approved Specialized BTV Reference (pending integration audit)

**Authority:** Subordinate to Creator decisions/corrections and Master Canon; specialized alongside Story & Lore, Character, and Covenant references.

**Status:** v1.0 — Initial Faith, Prayer & Armor reference. Unknown and proposed mechanics are deliberately preserved as open.

### Non-Invention Rule
Do not promote theological proposals, prayer mechanics, Armor mechanics, Bible passages, church practices, divine behavior, or supernatural rules into CANON without explicit creator approval.

## 1. Authority & Purpose
This document is the specialized BTV reference for faith, prayer, Scripture, spiritual guidance, the Armor of God, Christian worldview boundaries, and related narrative/gameplay principles. It deepens established canon without silently promoting UNKNOWN or PROPOSED material.

## 2. Authority Hierarchy
Creator decisions and corrections override all documents. The Master Canon remains the primary content authority. Story & Lore, Character, Covenant, and this Faith/Prayer/Armor reference are specialized documents. Conflicts must be surfaced, not silently resolved by invention.

## 3. Christian Worldview Foundation
BTV is an original modern Christian action-adventure survival RPG. Christianity is part of the world's worldview and meaning, not a cosmetic reskin. The game may depict spiritual conflict, faith, prayer, Scripture, service, redemption, perseverance, and discernment while remaining an original fictional work rather than a direct retelling of biblical narratives.

## 4. God
God is the ultimate authority in BTV. God is not a player-controlled combat character, collectible, upgrade tree, resource meter, or ordinary NPC whose power can be manipulated by the player. Divine action must not be reduced to a predictable gameplay transaction.

## 5. Christ
Christ remains above the Covenant, the Chosen, the player, and every game system. No character becomes divine, salvific, or a substitute for Christ. The Chosen's importance must never imply that the protagonist replaces Christ's role.

## 6. Holy Spirit
The Holy Spirit may be represented through guidance, conviction, warnings, wisdom, discernment, and direction. The Holy Spirit is not a controllable attack, mana pool, skill tree, summon, or weapon. Ember does not replace the Holy Spirit.

## 7. Faith
Faith should be portrayed as trust, perseverance, obedience, hope, conviction, and relationship with God rather than as a numerical stat. Faith is not directly purchasable, farmable, or spendable.

## 8. No Faith Currency
BTV must not introduce Faith Coins, Grace Points, Prayer Points, Salvation Tokens, spiritual XP, or similar currencies that imply spiritual realities can be bought or accumulated like ordinary resources. Aces and Covenant Credits remain ordinary in-world currencies and cannot purchase faith.

## 9. Prayer — Core Principle
Prayer is meaningful communication with God, not a vending machine or guaranteed spell. The player may pray in appropriate contexts, but outcomes are not mechanically guaranteed and may involve timing, circumstances, discernment, action, or unanswered uncertainty.

## 10. Prayer During Missions
Prayer can occur during or around missions when contextually appropriate. It may help frame decisions, fear, uncertainty, gratitude, repentance, courage, or dependence on God. Avoid turning prayer into a cooldown attack or guaranteed emergency button.

## 11. Prayer After Missions
Post-mission prayer may provide reflection on events, consequences, gratitude, grief, repentance, hope, or decisions. It should reinforce character and story rather than grant automatic rewards.

## 12. Prayer & Discernment
Prayer can accompany discernment but does not make the player omniscient. A player should not automatically receive perfect answers to mysteries merely by pressing a prayer button. Discernment may involve Scripture, circumstances, wisdom, counsel, evidence, and time.

## 13. Prayer Outcomes
Prayer outcomes must remain compatible with uncertainty and divine sovereignty. Avoid a deterministic formula such as stronger prayer = guaranteed better loot or victory. Exact prayer interaction design remains PROPOSED/OPEN.

## 14. Scripture — Core Principle
Scripture used in BTV must be handled accurately and respectfully. Scripture is not a magic incantation whose words mechanically force God to act. Its use should serve story, character, reflection, truth, encouragement, warning, or meaningful spiritual context.

## 15. Scripture in Gameplay
Scripture may appear in rare, deliberate cinematic or contextual moments, including declarations, conversations, church settings, prayer, reflection, or major confrontations. Existing considered passages include Philippians 4:13, Psalm 23, Psalm 91, Romans 8:31, and Armor of God passages; their exact uses remain subject to their established status.

## 16. Scripture Accuracy
Do not fabricate Bible quotations and present them as Scripture. If exact wording is required, verify it from an appropriate Bible translation/source. Avoid unnecessary quotation of copyrighted translations beyond permitted limits when producing external documentation.

## 17. Armor of God — Core Principle
The Armor of God is an established BTV thematic and visual framework: Belt of Truth, Breastplate of Righteousness, Shoes of the Gospel of Peace, Shield of Faith, Helmet of Salvation, and Sword of the Spirit. Exact gameplay mechanics remain OPEN unless separately approved.

## 18. Belt of Truth
Narratively, the Belt of Truth can represent truthfulness, integrity, resistance to deception, and commitment to what is true. It must not simply become an arbitrary damage-resistance stat.

## 19. Breastplate of Righteousness
Narratively, the Breastplate of Righteousness can represent righteous living, moral courage, integrity, and protection of the heart in a thematic sense. It should not imply that the player earns salvation by good deeds.

## 20. Shoes of the Gospel of Peace
Narratively, the Shoes can represent readiness, movement toward others, peace-making, service, outreach, and carrying the Gospel. Their gameplay expression must not reduce peace to a cosmetic bonus.

## 21. Shield of Faith
Narratively, the Shield represents trust in God amid fear, deception, pressure, and spiritual opposition. Its gameplay implementation must not imply that faith is a measurable ammunition supply.

## 22. Helmet of Salvation
Narratively, the Helmet represents salvation, identity, hope, and protection of the mind. It must not be treated as a purchasable item that grants salvation.

## 23. Sword of the Spirit
Narratively, the Sword of the Spirit is connected to the Word of God. Its depiction must preserve the distinction between Scripture and an ordinary fantasy weapon. Any combat mechanics are PROPOSED/OPEN and must not imply that Scripture is a magical damage spell.

## 24. Armor Visual Direction
The Armor of God should support a medieval-inspired but modern/tactical visual language established for BTV. The design should feel original, functional, Christian, and integrated with the Covenant aesthetic rather than copied from existing Christian media or fantasy franchises.

## 25. Armor Progression
Progressive Armor of God equipment is an established design direction, but exact acquisition order, upgrade rules, visual evolution, gameplay statistics, and narrative milestones remain OPEN unless explicitly approved.

## 26. Spiritual Warfare
BTV may depict spiritual warfare through deception, temptation, fear, corruption, influence, perseverance, truth, prayer, Scripture, courage, discernment, and resistance. Spiritual conflict should not be reduced to a simple good-stat-versus-evil-stat system.

## 27. Enemy Interaction
Destroyers, Persuaders, Deceivers, and Commanders/Bosses can challenge the player physically, psychologically, socially, or spiritually. Enemy classification describes story/game function, not complete metaphysical origin.

## 28. Deception & Discernment
Deceivers and Veil-related threats may create uncertainty, but BTV must maintain coherent rules. The player should have meaningful ways to investigate, compare evidence, observe behavior, seek counsel, and discern rather than receiving arbitrary answers.

## 29. Grace & Redemption
Grace and redemption should remain meaningful themes. Forgiveness, trust, reconciliation, restoration, repentance, and consequences are related but not automatically identical. Redemption does not erase every consequence.

## 30. Obedience & Moral Growth
Christian growth can include obedience, humility, courage, repentance, patience, service, perseverance, and learning from failure. BTV must not convert spiritual growth into a universal morality meter.

## 31. No Universal Morality Meter
There is no established universal morality meter that mechanically ranks the player's spiritual worth. Choices can have narrative and relational consequences without reducing Christianity to a score.

## 32. Evangelism & Outreach
Evangelism and outreach may appear through Messengers, church/community scenes, dialogue, service, missions, and character relationships. The player should not be forced into a shallow persuasion minigame that treats people as conversion statistics.

## 33. Church & Faith Community
Churches and faith communities are living parts of the world. They can contain worship, teaching, fellowship, service, grief, celebration, ordinary work, disagreement, and community support. The Covenant does not replace the Church.

## 34. HCI Relationship
HCI is a real-world inspiration for the game's church/community design. The degree of direct real-world institutional representation remains UNKNOWN and must not be invented. In-game church identity should follow the established canon once that decision is made.

## 35. Church Service
Church service is a world activity rather than merely a menu. NPCs may prepare, attend, worship, interact, and return to ordinary life. The exact service order remains UNKNOWN and requires creator approval.

## 36. KITFIT & Giving
The established KITFIT segment occurs before preaching and includes Scripture plus a short teaching/message about giving. Offerings and charity are voluntary. Generosity is not simply measured by amount, and wealth is not treated as moral worth. The exact expansion of KITFIT remains UNKNOWN.

## 37. Communion
The Communion Set combines bread and wine as one item and is used only inside a CommunionContextZone. First Sunday Communion is established. Communion is reflective/contextual and provides no buffs, XP, health, stat increase, or purchasable spiritual advantage.

## 38. Spiritual Guidance & Gameplay
Spiritual guidance should shape interpretation, choices, atmosphere, and narrative possibilities without becoming an omniscient navigation system. Guidance may be subtle, contextual, uncertain, or clarifying depending on story needs.

## 39. Faith & Character Development
Faith should influence character decisions, relationships, resilience, prayer life, humility, courage, service, and interpretation of events. Characters remain emotionally complex; faith does not make them flawless or emotionless.

## 40. Chosen & Faith
Asher Vance is human and must not be portrayed as divine. The Chosen's significance comes with responsibility rather than spiritual superiority. The dual-Chosen structure remains UNKNOWN and requires creator decision.

## 41. Covenant & Faith
Covenant membership is service, not a higher spiritual class. Covenant ranks must never be treated as levels of holiness. A General can be mistaken; a Recruit can demonstrate wisdom and faithfulness.

## 42. Ember & Faith
Ember's spiritual development is established as a development dimension, while exact nature and origin remain UNKNOWN. Ember can support the story's spiritual themes but does not replace God, Christ, Scripture, prayer, or the Holy Spirit.

## 43. Faith & Economy
Faith cannot be bought, sold, traded, or converted into ordinary currency. Aces and Covenant Credits remain economic systems with no direct spiritual equivalence.

## 44. Faith System Design Boundaries
Do not implement prayer as a spell list, faith as mana, Scripture as a damage cheat code, salvation as loot, grace as currency, Holy Spirit guidance as a controllable attack, or church/Communion as stat farms.

## 45. Open Gameplay Mechanics
Prayer interaction, Armor statistics, exact progression, combat expression of the Sword of the Spirit, Shield mechanics, visual upgrade stages, and other faith-related gameplay systems remain OPEN/PROPOSED until separately designed and approved.

## 46. Research & Biblical Accuracy
When BTV makes claims about Scripture, Christian doctrine, church practice, or historical Christian concepts, research should prioritize reliable primary or authoritative sources. Research informs accuracy but does not automatically become BTV canon.

## 47. Originality
BTV must create its own fictional systems, terminology, narrative structures, visual designs, and gameplay implementations. Biblical concepts may inspire the game, but existing games, films, ministries, or fictional universes must not be copied.

## 48. AI Development Rule
AI must never invent theological canon, Bible quotations, spiritual mechanics, divine promises, church doctrine, or supernatural rules and present them as established BTV facts. Proposals must be explicitly labeled PROPOSED and remain subject to creator approval.

## 49. Faith Feature Proposal Template
For a proposed faith-related feature record: name; biblical/thematic basis; narrative purpose; gameplay purpose; player interaction; theological boundary; risks of misunderstanding; dependencies; status; open questions; implementation considerations; canon audit.

## 50. Faith Canon Audit
Every major faith, prayer, Scripture, Armor, church, or spiritual-warfare feature must be checked against creator decisions, Master Canon, Story & Lore Bible, Character Bible, Covenant Bible, originality requirements, and the no-invention rule.

## 51. Current Faith/Prayer/Armor Unknown Register
Unknown/open areas include exact prayer UI and interaction, prayer outcome model, Armor acquisition/progression, Armor gameplay statistics, Sword of the Spirit combat implementation, exact Scripture passages and cinematic placement, exact church service order, KITFIT acronym expansion, HCI representation degree, Ember's exact spiritual nature, and any deeper metaphysical rules not established by higher authority.

## 52. Future Specialized Documents
Possible subordinate references include a Scripture & Biblical References Guide, Armor of God Design/Equipment Bible, Church & Worship World Bible, Spiritual Warfare & Veil Rules Manual, Prayer Interaction Specification, or Christian Theology Research Notes. These are roadmap possibilities, not current canon.

## 53. Final Faith Principle
Faith in BTV should feel meaningful without becoming a currency, spell system, score, or shortcut. Prayer should feel like prayer, Scripture should remain Scripture, the Armor of God should retain its meaning, spiritual conflict should remain coherent, and Christ must remain above every fictional system BTV creates.
