# BEYOND THE VEIL: THE LAST WATCH
## CHURCH / HCI / SERVICE / COMMUNION BIBLE v1.0

**Document Type:** Approved Specialized BTV Reference (pending integration audit)

**Authority:** Subordinate to Creator decisions/corrections and Master Canon; domain-specialized alongside Story & Lore, Character, Covenant, and Faith/Prayer/Armor references.

**Status:** v1.0 — Initial Church/HCI/Service/Communion reference. Unresolved representation, service-order, and gameplay questions remain open.

### Non-Invention Rule
Do not invent official HCI identities, endorsements, church leadership, service order, KITFIT expansion, church doctrine, named congregations, or Communion mechanics beyond established canon. Label proposals explicitly.

## 1. Authority & Purpose
Specialized BTV reference for churches, HCI inspiration, chapel/community life, worship/service, KITFIT, giving, Communion, church NPCs, and church/Covenant relationships. It deepens established canon without silently promoting unresolved material.

## 2. Authority & Status
Creator decisions/corrections override all references. Master Canon is primary content authority. Other specialized Bibles govern their domains. Unknowns remain unknown until explicitly decided.

## 3. Church in BTV
Churches are living communities, not menus or passive scenery. They contain worship, teaching, fellowship, service, ordinary work, relationships, celebration, grief, disagreement, and care.

## 4. Church Is Not the Covenant
The Church and Covenant have different identities and purposes. The Covenant serves and protects; it does not replace the Church or possess automatic spiritual superiority.

## 5. Church Is Not a Gameplay Faction
Churches should not be reduced to faction reputation, vendors, buff stations, or resource farms. Their importance comes from people, worship, service, meaning, and community.

## 6. HCI Inspiration
Harvest Chapel International (HCI) is an established real-world inspiration for BTV's church/community design. The degree of direct institutional representation remains UNKNOWN.

## 7. HCI Representation Boundary
Do not invent official in-game HCI identities, leadership, doctrine, policies, locations, endorsements, or institutional claims. Direct representation requires explicit creator approval.

## 8. Chapel Identity
In-game chapels should communicate Christian identity while remaining original BTV spaces. Architecture, materials, layout, decoration, music, and community activity may vary by location.

## 9. Regional Variation
Chapel architecture and community expression can vary naturally by region, settlement, resources, history, climate, and population without implying spiritual superiority.

## 10. Living Congregations
Congregations should feel inhabited. NPCs may arrive, greet one another, worship, listen, serve, help visitors, prepare spaces, leave, work, and continue ordinary life.

## 11. Population Composition
Public/community spaces should contain substantially more civilians, families, workers, visitors, worshippers, and merchants than Covenant personnel unless explicitly a Covenant facility or crisis area.

## 12. Church Roles
Possible roles include pastors/ministers, worship workers, volunteers, ushers, teachers, youth/family workers, charity helpers, musicians, visitors, elders, and ordinary members. Specific named individuals remain UNKNOWN.

## 13. Church Characterization
Faithful characters remain emotionally complex. Joy, grief, doubt, courage, fatigue, disagreement, hope, humor, and uncertainty can all exist without emotion itself being treated as spiritual failure.

## 14. Service as World Activity
Church service should be experienced as a world activity. NPC preparation, arrival, worship, teaching, fellowship, giving, and departure can form believable routines.

## 15. Exact Service Order
The exact BTV church service order remains UNKNOWN and requires creator approval. Do not invent a locked sequence as established canon.

## 16. Service Purpose
Service scenes can communicate worship, teaching, fellowship, reflection, encouragement, correction, and community identity. They should not exist solely to distribute player rewards.

## 17. Pre-Service Life
Before service, NPCs may prepare spaces, greet people, arrange equipment, converse, help visitors, or complete ordinary tasks.

## 18. KITFIT Status
KITFIT is an established pre-preaching segment involving Scripture and a short teaching/message about giving. The exact acronym expansion remains UNKNOWN.

## 19. Giving Principles
Giving is voluntary. Do not equate larger amounts with greater righteousness or treat wealth as proof of spiritual worth.

## 20. Charity & Community Support
Giving and charity can support community needs, relief, restoration, or outreach in narrative contexts. Exact economic mechanics remain OPEN.

## 21. No Giving-for-Blessing Mechanic
Do not create a system in which donating more guarantees blessings, loot, healing, XP, luck, or divine favor.

## 22. Communion Status
Communion is an established contextual activity. The Communion Set contains bread and wine as one item and is usable only inside a CommunionContextZone.

## 23. First Sunday Communion
First Sunday Communion is established. Exact presentation, service order, dialogue, and congregation details remain subject to approved design.

## 24. Communion Has No Buffs
Communion provides no health restoration, XP, stat increase, combat buff, currency, equipment enhancement, or purchasable spiritual advantage.

## 25. Communion Tone
Communion should be reverent and contextual, not ordinary loot, a collectible, a minigame reward, or a supernatural consumable.

## 26. Communion & Community
Communion belongs within church/community context rather than functioning as isolated player power.

## 27. Church & Scripture
Scripture may appear in services, teaching, prayer, reflection, signage, or conversations. Scripture must be accurate and never fabricated.

## 28. Church & Prayer
Prayer may occur individually or collectively. It remains non-transactional and is not a guaranteed spell, cooldown, or reward.

## 29. Church & Evangelism
Evangelism/outreach may appear through conversation, teaching, service, hospitality, community work, and Messenger-related activity. People are not conversion statistics.

## 30. Church & Redemption
Church communities can participate in repentance, forgiveness, reconciliation, restoration, grief, support, and second chances. Redemption does not automatically erase consequences.

## 31. Church During Crisis
Churches may shelter people, organize aid, pray, provide food/support, communicate information, help families, and support recovery. Exact crisis-response systems remain OPEN.

## 32. Church & Veil Events
Veil anomalies may affect communities and create investigation, fear, confusion, or service opportunities. Churches do not automatically possess complete supernatural knowledge.

## 33. Church & Covenant Cooperation
Covenant personnel may protect, assist, investigate near, communicate with, or serve churches when appropriate. Cooperation does not make the Covenant the church's governing authority.

## 34. Church & Government
Churches and governments are distinct institutions. Their relationships may vary naturally. Exact BTV legal, political, and institutional structures remain UNKNOWN.

## 35. Festivals & Calendar Events
Easter, Christmas, and other Christian/community festivals may appear as world events. Exact implementation, frequency, service content, and significance remain PROPOSED/OPEN unless approved.

## 36. Ordinary Church Life
Church life should include conversations, families, children, work, cleaning, preparation, visitors, announcements, meals, volunteering, and community care.

## 37. Church Architecture & Visual Design
Chapel design should be original and regionally responsive. Avoid copying recognizable real-world buildings or another game's church design.

## 38. Audio & Atmosphere
Church spaces may use environmental sound, music, speech, congregation activity, silence, and ambient life. Exact music systems and compositions remain OPEN.

## 39. NPC Routines
Church NPCs should have plausible routines, schedules, relationships, and reasons for being present. They are not omniscient quest databases.

## 40. Church-Related Missions
Potential missions include community help, rescue, outreach, restoration, investigation, protection, aid, missing persons, crisis response, or reconciliation. Avoid repetitive fetch/kill loops.

## 41. Church Rewards
Church/community participation should not automatically generate spiritual stats or divine currency. Material rewards, if any, should arise naturally from service, relationships, practical help, or mission context.

## 42. Respect & Player Agency
Players should not be manipulated by artificial pressure. Voluntary giving and contextual worship are especially important.

## 43. Church Knowledge Boundaries
Church workers and members know what their roles and experiences plausibly allow. They do not automatically know Covenant secrets or the Veil's deepest rules.

## 44. HCI Safety & Originality
Real-world inspiration informs principles, not fabricated official claims. BTV must maintain its own fictional identity and must not imply endorsement or partnership without explicit authorization.

## 45. Church Feature Proposal Template
Record: name; location/context; community purpose; worship/service purpose; narrative purpose; gameplay purpose; NPC roles; theological boundary; HCI impact; dependencies; status; open questions; canon audit.

## 46. Church Canon Audit
Major church, HCI, service, giving, Communion, festival, or community features must be checked against creator decisions, Master Canon, Story & Lore, Character, Covenant, Faith/Prayer/Armor, originality, and no-invention rules.

## 47. Current Church/HCI Unknown Register
UNKNOWN/open: HCI representation degree; named congregations and leaders; exact service order; KITFIT acronym; chapel locations; detailed festival schedules; giving/charity mechanics; church crisis-response systems.

## 48. Future Specialized Documents
Possible subordinate references include Church Architecture & Environment Bible, Worship/Service Specification, HCI Representation Guide, Community & Civilian Life Bible, Festival/Calendar Bible, Church NPC & Routine Manual, and Charity/Outreach Mission Guide. These are roadmap possibilities, not current canon.

## 49. Final Church Principle
Churches in BTV should feel like living communities rather than game mechanics, expressing worship, fellowship, service, truth, care, ordinary life, grief, celebration, and hope while remaining distinct from the Covenant and faithful to BTV's Christian boundaries.
