# BEYOND THE VEIL: THE LAST WATCH
## Ridgeview Outpost — Dialogue & Mission Content Pack v0.1

**Date:** 1 November 2026  
**Status:** PROPOSED authored content, based on the APPROVED DESIGN direction in Ridgeview Case Design v0.2  
**Scope:** Vertical-slice dialogue, evidence text, mission beats and report templates  
**Authority:** Locked Vertical Slice Specification v1.0 controls. This pack does not promote new dialogue or case details to global canon.

---

## 0. Production rules

1. Keep all dialogue source-bounded. A character may say only what they know, believe, infer or have been told.
2. Do not name or identify the Devourers anywhere in player-facing Ridgeview slice content: dialogue, objectives, subtitles, UI, Journal, Covenant classification or Asher's report.
3. Do not connect Ridgeview to Marek Sloane or the Concordance without a separately approved evidence chain. No such connection is established here.
4. The repairman remains missing and not confirmed dead. Do not write a death confirmation, rescue reveal, captivity reveal or final location.
5. Pike is not a one-dimensional villain. He has made harmful choices and is responsible for them; his fear may explain, but not excuse, those choices.
6. Wren is a frightened resident who wants out. She is not an exposition machine and is not responsible for the whole crisis.
7. No dialogue choice guarantees conversion, confession, forgiveness, safety or a supernatural answer.
8. Psalm 23 remains the single authored threshold moment defined by the Vertical Slice Specification. Do not reproduce its full text in this pack.
9. No gore, graphic injury, remains, explicit violence or frightening detail beyond the approved non-graphic presentation.
10. These lines are draft voice targets. Final performance, localisation and age-appropriate content review remain required.

## 2. Pike dialogue pack

### 2.1 First contact — RV-PK-01

**Pike:** “You came a long way to ask questions. I hope the road wasn't too difficult.”

**Asher — neutral:** “I'm checking on the outpost. The road has been quiet.”

**Pike:** “It has. Deliveries have been irregular. People have adjusted.”

**Asher — ask about residents:** “There are still people here.”

**Pike:** “Of course. We haven't vanished. We keep to our routines where we can.”

**Asher — ask about the supply delivery:** “The report says a delivery was missed.”

**Pike:** “It was. The road has not been reliable. We have managed what we can.”

**Asher — ask about the repairman:** “A repair worker was expected. Do you know where he is?”

**Pike:** “He came to look at the equipment. I can't tell you where he went after that.”

**Asher — leave:** “I'll look around before I draw conclusions.”

**Pike:** “That's sensible. Just don't mistake every rumour for a fact.”

*Knowledge notes:* Pike knows residents remain, deliveries were disrupted, the repairman attended and went toward the camp. The initial dialogue intentionally omits his concealment. No line names the group or explains the Veil.

### 2.2 Listen-first branch — RV-PK-02

**Asher:** “What changed first?”

**Pike:** “The ordinary things. A delivery late. A radio check missed. Then people started asking questions I couldn't answer.”

**Asher:** “What did you tell them?”

**Pike:** “That we would manage. I thought keeping everyone calm would help.”

**Asher:** “Did it?”

**Pike:** “For a while. Or I wanted to believe it did.”

*Branch effect:* Opens a more candid follow-up. It does not set Pike to cooperative or prove he has told the whole truth.

### 2.3 Evidence challenge — RV-PK-03

**Trigger:** Asher presents a verified discrepancy in the work/supply record or a supported contradiction between Pike's statement and physical evidence.

**Asher:** “This record doesn't match what you told me.”

**Pike:** “You don't know what was happening here when that was written.”

**Asher:** “Then help me understand it.”

**Pike:** “I was trying to keep the settlement together. People were frightened. Every answer brought another question.”

**Asher:** “Keeping people calm isn't the same as telling them the truth.”

**Pike:** “No. It isn't.”

*Optional Pike continuation:* “I thought I could contain it by keeping it quiet. I was wrong about what that would cost.”

*Branch effect:* Records a bounded admission only if the player presented the supporting evidence. It does not equal a full confession or legal finding.

### 2.4 Wren testimony challenge — RV-PK-04

**Trigger:** Asher mentions Wren's account without disclosing information she asked him to keep private, unless she explicitly authorised disclosure in the scene.

**Pike:** “Wren is frightened. She has had a difficult time.”

**Asher:** “That doesn't make everything she says false.”

**Pike:** “I didn't say it did.”

**Asher:** “Then answer the part you can answer. Why are people being kept away from the camp?”

**Pike:** “Because I thought it was safer if fewer people went there.”

**Asher:** “Safer for whom?”

**Pike:** “I don't have a good answer.”

*Branch effect:* Establishes Pike's limited admission if the relevant evidence is present. The game must not disclose Wren's confidential details without her permission.

### 2.5 Speak/Preach branch — RV-PK-05

**Asher:** “Fear can make silence feel like protection. But people still need the truth to make their own choices.”

**Pike:** “And what if the truth puts them in danger?”

**Asher:** “Then we should help them face the danger together. Hiding it from them doesn't make it disappear.”

**Pike:** “You make it sound simple.”

**Asher:** “It isn't simple. But people deserve honesty, and they deserve help.”

*Optional authored Scripture context:* Use only a separately approved passage and context. No automatic verse selection, no fabricated quotation and no forced conversion branch.

*Branch effect:* May keep dialogue open or cause Pike to end the conversation. Does not alter faith, salvation, spiritual worth or a hidden conversion variable.

### 2.6 Pike exit lines — RV-PK-06

- **If Asher leaves after limited evidence:** “You have your questions. I have people to look after.”
- **If Asher presents strong evidence:** “I can't make that record say something else.”
- **If Asher threatens him:** “If you came here to force an answer, you won't get one from me.”
- **If Asher offers help:** “Help the people who want to leave. Don't promise what you can't deliver.”

These are alternative authored lines, not a random personality system. The chosen line must reflect actual prior dialogue and evidence.

## 3. Wren dialogue pack

### 3.1 Initial signal — RV-WR-01

*Wren waits until she can speak without drawing attention. She does not grab Asher or force the interaction.*

**Wren:** “Could I speak with you somewhere quieter?”

**Asher:** “Yes. We can take our time.”

**Wren:** “Not here. People notice who asks what.”

**Asher:** “Are you in immediate danger?”

**Wren:** “I don't know. I just don't want to stay.”

### 3.2 Establishing what she knows — RV-WR-02

**Asher:** “Tell me what you saw. Start with what you know for certain.”

**Wren:** “I saw the repairman go toward the camp. I didn't follow him.”

**Asher:** “When?”

**Wren:** “After he argued about the records. I can't give you the exact time.”

**Asher:** “Did you see him come back?”

**Wren:** “No.”

**Asher:** “Do you know what happened to him?”

**Wren:** “No. That's what scares me.”

*Knowledge note:* Wren's direct observation is that she saw him go toward the camp. She did not witness what happened afterward. Her fear is not evidence of a particular fate.

### 3.3 Her part in the concealment — RV-WR-03

**Asher:** “Why didn't you tell anyone?”

**Wren:** “I was told to stop asking. Then I was asked to keep other people from asking. I did some of it.”

**Asher:** “Why?”

**Wren:** “I was afraid of what would happen if I refused. I told myself it was only for a little while.”

**Asher:** “You can tell me what happened without pretending you did nothing.”

**Wren:** “I know. I should have spoken sooner.”

*Branch effect:* Wren acknowledges limited participation. Do not make her the architect of the crisis or treat her fear as proof of innocence in every matter.

### 3.4 Rescue request — RV-WR-04

**Wren:** “I want to leave. I don't know if anyone else will come.”

**Asher:** “I won't promise what I can't guarantee. I can help you find a safer way out.”

**Wren:** “What about the others?”

**Asher:** “We'll consider who wants help and what we can safely do. I won't force anyone.”

*Branch effect:* Rescue planning begins only if the player chooses it and the route conditions are met. Wren's consent remains explicit.

### 3.5 If Asher cannot rescue her now — RV-WR-05

**Asher:** “I can't get you out safely yet. I won't pretend otherwise.”

**Wren:** “Then tell someone what is happening. Don't let this disappear into another report.”

*Branch effect:* Records her actual state. No false “rescued” flag. If Asher withdraws, he reports that Wren requested help and remains at risk if that is what occurred.

## 4. Resident dialogue fragments

Residents should have short, differentiated accounts, not repeat identical exposition.

### Resident A — routine disruption

“I used to know when the supply vehicle would arrive. Lately, nobody gives a straight answer. I can tell you when I last saw it, but not why it stopped.”

### Resident B — radio reliability

“The radio cuts in and out. Once I heard someone answer, but I couldn't make out who it was. I won't pretend I know.”

### Resident C — camp avoidance

“People don't go out there now. I haven't been there myself. That's all I can honestly tell you.”

### Resident D — Pike's reputation

“Pike kept things running for years. Some people still trust him. Others think he should have told us more. I don't know the whole story.”

*Implementation note:* Use a small number of authored resident roles with different knowledge flags. Do not make all residents repeat the same line or share a collective memory.

### 5.1 Journal wording rules

- Keep the original Scout Report label visible as historical provenance.
- Add Asher's local status separately: Confirmed, Contradicted or Still open.
- Use Unverified only for a player-created link that has not been supported.
- Never upgrade a report because a high-ranking NPC repeats it.
- Never mark a hypothesis Confirmed because Ember reacted to a location.

## 6. Evidence Board prompts

The Board may offer neutral relation prompts, not automatic answers:

- “These records refer to the same work period. Compare their dates and sources.”
- “This testimony describes a direction of travel, not what happened afterward.”
- “The settlement's current occupancy contradicts the trader's claim about it being shut for weeks.”
- “This connection is still Unverified. What independent evidence supports it?”
- “These two accounts differ. Check whether they refer to the same time and event.”

The Board must not offer:
- “The Devourers did this.”
- “Pike is secretly [enemy class].”
- “The Veil caused the missing person.”
- “Ember knows the answer.”
- “God is telling you to choose this option.”

## 7. Player objective text

Objectives describe actions and questions, not unsupported conclusions.

- “Compare the Scout Report with the approach to Ridgeview.”
- “Establish whether anyone remains at the outpost.”
- “Find out what can be verified about the missed delivery.”
- “Speak with Wren if she is willing.”
- “Ask Dorian Pike about the repairman's visit.”
- “Follow the available evidence toward the outlying camp.”
- “Decide how to respond with the information you have.”
- “Submit an accurate report to the Covenant.”

Avoid “Eliminate the Devourers,” “Find the portal,” “Save the repairman” or “Expose the Concordance” in this slice. Those statements exceed the locked information and mission scope.

## 8. Final report content templates

The report builder selects from authored claim records and only permits labels supported by Asher's actual evidence.

### 8.1 Full investigation — sample structure

**Area:** Ridgeview Outpost and outlying camp.  
**Observed:** Residents remain at the settlement; the trader's claim that the outpost had been shut for weeks is contradicted. The approach was unusually quiet. The repairman's trail points toward the camp.  
**Reported:** Wren stated that she saw the repairman go toward the camp. Other residents described disruptions to routine and communications.  
**Inferred:** The camp is connected to the settlement's restricted movement and the repairman's last known trail. Pike withheld material information.  
**Unconfirmed:** The repairman's current location and fate; the full identity and purpose of the group applying pressure; the exact cause of the local signal and environmental irregularities.  
**Recommendation:** Request proportionate follow-up based on whether Wren was assisted, what evidence was preserved and whether an immediate threat remains.

This is a template. The game must remove any sentence whose evidence condition was not met.

### 8.2 Partial investigation / withdrawal

**Observed:** Include only locations and events Asher personally observed.  
**Reported:** Include testimony actually heard, with speaker and context.  
**Inferred:** Include only cautious conclusions supported by collected evidence.  
**Unconfirmed:** Explicitly list missed or unresolved matters that materially affect the case.  
**Recommendation:** Request further assessment or assistance; do not claim a rescue or resolution that did not occur.

### 8.3 Covenant reply variants

- **Evidence strong, outcome partial:** “Your report establishes a serious concern, but not the full sequence. Preserve the records and do not fill the gaps with assumptions. We'll arrange follow-up.”
- **Wren rescued:** “Confirm her immediate needs and ensure she is not returned to an unsafe situation. The repairman's status remains open.”
- **Early withdrawal:** “Acknowledged. You withdrew before confirming the camp. Your observations are still useful. We'll reassess the risk before assigning another team.”
- **Pike made a bounded admission:** “Record exactly what he admitted and what evidence supports it. Acknowledging one fact does not establish full cooperation or erase the consequences of earlier choices.”

No reply grants promotion, rewards or a faith-related benefit.

## 9. Branch-state mapping

Use existing `ridgeview.*` flags from the Vertical Slice Specification. Do not create new flags solely to store dialogue flavour.

| Dialogue/content event | Existing state destination | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Wren asks to leave and Asher helps | `wren_outcome` | Set only after actual departure to safety |
| Wren speaks but is not moved | `wren_outcome=stayed` or `unknown` as appropriate | Do not equate conversation with rescue |
| Pike is challenged with evidence | `pike_outcome` | Use only the supported existing enum value; a bounded admission belongs in the report/evidence record, not a new cooperation flag |
| Repairman trail discovered | `repairman_trail` | `trail_found` or `trail_followed`; status remains unresolved |
| Evidence is collected | `evidence_documented` | Store evidence IDs actually acquired |
| Claims are assessed | `claims_resolved` | Each claim is confirmed, contradicted or open |
| Player uses approaches | `approaches_used` | Add only approaches actually used |
| Combat occurs | `combat_occurred` | Record true only if combat actually happened |
| Covenant follow-up selected | `covenant_followup` | Must match report and outcome |
| Exit state | `veil_state_at_exit` | Disturbed or Influenced, based on actual zone reached |

If dialogue needs a temporary local variable, keep it transient unless it affects a later authored scene. The existing `pike_outcome` enum has no dedicated cooperation value; do not invent one silently. A bounded admission can be preserved as sourced report content. Any persistent cooperation state requires a separately reviewed schema change. Do not create an affinity, guilt, faith or conversion score.
