# 1. Canon and scope

**[LOCKED] The slice.** Asher Vance's first Sunday Communion at Logos Rhema Temple, then a Covenant alert, a Scout Report, his review of the evidence, a Covenant vehicle journey, the Ridgeview approach, an on-foot investigation, his response, and his own final report.

**[LOCKED] Authority.** Creator decisions, then the Master Canon, then the specialized Bibles. This document is subordinate to them and flags conflicts rather than resolving them silently.

**[SLICE] What the slice must prove.** BTV can feel like a living world before danger arrives. Investigation is about weighing evidence. A disturbing threat can be told without gore. Choices carry weight without a morality meter. Faith shapes how Asher acts without becoming a mechanic.

**[LOCKED] Core locks.** Asher is the sole protagonist and Chosen, an Agent/Specialist and a Watcher. Ember is his companion, with five abilities and seven official commands. Real HCI is inspiration only; Logos Rhema Temple is the fictional church. The congregation does not know Asher is Covenant. Ridgeview content boundaries are locked, the Devourers are never named, and Psalm 23 is used at Ridgeview. No response choice earns XP or a morality score.

**Out of scope.** Other regions, the full combat model, the full companion, communication and world_states systems, and anything not named in this specification.

# 2. Character definitions

**[LOCKED] Asher Vance.** Sole playable protagonist and Chosen, aged 16 to 19 (presented as 18). Agent/Specialist rank, Watcher branch. Human, never divine. A trained field operative who can defend himself and protect civilians.

**[LOCKED] Ember.** Asher's established companion, an intelligent animal and not a weapon.

**[LOCKED] Logos Rhema leadership (original fictional characters, none portraying real HCI people):**

- **Pastor Elias Warrick**, Senior Pastor, 61. Unhurried, dry humor, a former history teacher.
- **Pastor Maribel Santos**, Associate Pastor, 38. Warm and direct, a former hospital nurse.
- **Tobias Reed**, Worship Leader, 29. Gentle and earnest, a former session musician.
- **Deacon Amos Whitaker**, Communion Steward, 72. Quiet and precise, a retired baker.
- **Leah Okoye**, Youth and Community Ministry Leader, 26. Energetic, grew up in the church's youth group.

**[LOCKED] Ridgeview.** **Dorian Pike**, the local leader, and **Wren**, a frightened resident who wants out (Section 13).

**[LOCKED] Unnamed by design.** The Scout who sent the report, the Covenant duty officer, the visitor, and the repairman. Their names are UNKNOWN. Do not invent them without approval.

**[RESERVED] Not in this slice.** Ayla, the human Covenant Shepherd, and any other teammate.

# 3. Covenant structure

**[LOCKED] Ranks.** Recruit, Agent/Specialist (one tier), Team Leader, Commander, General. Rank means responsibility and trust, never closeness to God. **Branches:** Guardians, Messengers, Watchers, Shepherds, Scouts, Keepers.

**[LOCKED] Asher's position.** Agent/Specialist and Watcher are separate concepts. He is not a Team Leader. Promotion is earned through story, judgment, responsibility and consequence, never XP grinding. There is no promotion in this slice.

**[LOCKED] Secrecy.** The Covenant is hidden. Only appropriate Covenant personnel and systems know Asher's affiliation. The church is never a Covenant space.

**[SLICE] Authority at his tier.** Asher acts on a Covenant assignment and uses field judgment. He may protect life in immediate danger and may withdraw. Larger decisions are recommendations to the Covenant.

**[LOCKED] Oath.** "By truth we stand. By faith we endure. By the Light we rise. Darkness will not prevail." Not used in this slice unless a scene earns it.

# 4. Logos Rhema Sunday

**[LOCKED] Nature.** A living world activity, not a menu or checklist. Flexible structure: Arrival and Fellowship, Welcome, Praise and Worship, Prayer, Scripture Reading, KITFIT, Offering, Sermon, Response and Reflection, Communion, Announcements, Closing Prayer, Fellowship and Service.

**[LOCKED] Scripture.** Philippians 4:13 (NIV) in Phase 3, used in context. The text is never reproduced in documentation.

**[LOCKED] Offering.** Voluntary. Aces may be given. No leaderboard, pressure, guilt, reward for giving, penalty for not giving, or amount visible to other NPCs.

**[LOCKED] Ember at church.** He accompanies Asher to the grounds and never disrupts worship or Communion. He is not a worshipper, spiritual authority or religious symbol.

**[LOCKED] Rewards.** Attending earns no XP, Aces, Credits, equipment, stats or progression, and creates no forced objectives. Lingering is free of penalty.

**[SLICE] Design.**

- **Agency.** No timer, no objective marker pushing the player through. Asher may arrive early, talk, help, sit, step outside quietly, or skip a segment.
- **Ember.** Waits at a designated spot with a clear line to Asher, such as a covered side porch, under Hold. Greetings from children and members only before and after the service.
- **Population.** Civilians to Covenant roughly 10 to 1 or higher, as a tunable data value.
- **Scripture use.** Philippians 4:13 is read with its context of contentment in every circumstance, so it reads as endurance and not winning. No declaration mechanic.
- **Visitor.** In Phase 1 or Phase 10 she mentions one relative who went to work in a remote area and has missed check-ins. She does not name Ridgeview or explain anything, and she holds no Covenant secrets.
- **Look.** Original, fictional, warm, community-oriented, regionally adaptable, never like a Covenant facility, and copying no real HCI building.

# 5. Communion

**[LOCKED]** First Sunday Communion. Asher uses the **Communion Set** (bread and wine as one item) only inside the **CommunionContextZone**. Outside it the contextual line is: "This is reserved for an appropriate Communion gathering." The sequence is Prepare, Receive, Reflect, Pray, Continue.

**[LOCKED] No rewards of any kind:** no XP, Aces, Credits, equipment, stat increase, health increase, protection buff or progression. It is a sacred narrative and reflection moment. Faith is never a stat, prayer is never transactional, and the Holy Spirit is not a mechanic.

**[SLICE]** Deacon Amos Whitaker guides it in few words. The scene ends in reflection, not a result screen.

# 6. Covenant alert

**[LOCKED]** The alert is available only after the service ends. It never interrupts worship, prayer, Scripture, KITFIT, offering, sermon, Communion or closing prayer. Asher can linger at Logos Rhema without penalty. The congregation neither sees nor learns of the alert.

**[SLICE]** It is concerning, not an emergency, so the Covenant Link holds it in a queue and delivers it as a quiet vibration only Asher notices, once the ServiceDirector finishes. Asher chooses when to open it, and no HUD marker appears until he does. Opening it grants nothing.

# 7. Scout Report

**[LOCKED] Source.** The Scout who gathered the information sends the report through the Covenant's reporting system. A duty officer may receive or forward it but does not replace the Scout as the source.

**[LOCKED] Confidence categories** (they describe the strength of the evidence, not absolute truth): **Observed, Reported, Inferred, Unconfirmed.** "Unverified" is reserved for connections the player draws on the Evidence Board.

**[LOCKED] Rules.** Real intelligence, not a quest marker. Uncertain and incomplete. It never names, identifies or hints at the Devourers. Asher cannot magically know more than the Covenant has established. Reading it rewards nothing.

**[LOCKED] Planted error.** A harmless second-hand mistake: a roadside trader says the outpost has been shut for weeks. Asher can disprove it on site.

**[SLICE] Contents.** Source, approximate area (not an exact pin), observed facts, reported claims, inferences, alternative explanations, gaps, a non-binding recommendation.

| Claim | Label |
|---|---|
| No vehicle traffic on the approach road for two days | Observed |
| Only one building lit at night | Observed |
| People are staying in that building | Inferred |
| A radio check was answered once by an unfamiliar voice that ended the call | Observed |
| The supply delivery was missed, and the outpost has been shut for weeks | Reported (trader), Unconfirmed |
| Two travelers are overdue, one a man on a repair contract | Reported, Unconfirmed |
| Birdsong and small wildlife are unusually absent | Observed, cause Unconfirmed |

The Scout also lists ordinary explanations (storm damage, a power or radio failure, a seasonal slowdown) and says plainly that they did not enter and cannot confirm anyone is in danger.

# 8. Travel to Ridgeview

**[LOCKED]** A practical Covenant field vehicle, not futuristic or overpowered, carries Asher and Ember to an access point near Ridgeview. He continues on foot. The journey is not a forced loading-screen-only fast travel.

**[SLICE]** The vehicle is plain with discreet identification, in keeping with a hidden organization. The ride is guided along a path with a free camera, ambient radio contact, and a real stop at a screened pull-off. Arrival is late afternoon moving into evening, so the report's night lights can be tested. The Covenant Link weakens past the ridge.

# 9. Ridgeview investigation

**[LOCKED] Principle.** Ridgeview is where Asher tests the report against reality. It is an established Devourers mission setting: an isolated settlement, missing people, a leader to talk to, evidence to find, and a decision to make.

**[SLICE] Structure (order is a guide; the player may skip, reorder and revisit).**

1. **Approach and perimeter.** A watched road, abandoned belongings, a parked work vehicle with a toolkit inside, the start of the repairman's trail.
2. **The settlement.** People are present, which disproves the trader's claim. Residents are wary and answer if Asher listens first.
3. **Wren.** She signals she wants to talk away from the others.
4. **The outlying camp.** Residents avoid mentioning it. It holds the hardest evidence about what became of the repairman, but no full explanation.
5. **Pike.** A conversation Asher can open by listening, pressing with evidence, preaching, or leaving.
6. **Synthesis.** Asher organizes findings on the Evidence Board.
7. **Decision.** The five response options (Section 15).

**[SLICE] Veil stages.** The approach reads as Disturbed (missing wildlife, muffled sound, Ember more alert). The settlement center and camp read as Influenced (unreliable radio and devices, uneasy residents, a quiet that deepens toward the camp). The learnable rule is that influence shows as quiet and unreliability, intensifying toward the camp. It creates no monsters here and never excuses human choices. Veil-Touched, Breach and Critical are not reached, and what the Veil is doing stays unexplained.

**[LOCKED] Psalm 23.** A rare, authored declaration of faith, courage, trust and reliance on God, spoken by Asher when the story has earned it. It is not a combat spell, damage, buff or healing, grants nothing, guarantees no outcome, and cannot be repeated or bought. The full text is not reproduced in documentation.

**[SLICE] Placement.** A threshold moment as Asher chooses to go on into the camp area with the stakes understood. It is triggered by story state, not by a button, appears once, and changes only the scene: tone, Ember's reaction, and what a nearby witness hears. It never alters stats or combat. **Open:** the translation is not named (Section 23).

# 10. Watcher toolkit

**[LOCKED] Identity.** Watchers do investigation, intelligence, observation, evidence assessment, threat identification and discernment. Asher is still a capable field operative, may cross-train and cooperate with other branches, and does not belong to all of them.

**[SLICE] Tools.**

- **Careful observation.** A slow, deliberate look that surfaces details worth noting, but not what they mean. It costs time and exposure and is not x-ray vision.
- **Evidence assessment.** Tag how strong a piece of evidence is, in step with the four labels.
- **Threat read.** Interpret positions, tension and behavior from what is visible. It reads behavior, not minds.
- **Cross-checking.** Compare one account against another.
- **Limits.** Watchers do not automatically know hidden Veil truths. Other branches' skills appear only in basic form: self-defense and protecting civilians.

# 11. Ember systems

**[LOCKED] Commands.** Attack, Protect, Scout, Track, Retrieve, Hold, Regroup. "Stay" is legacy wording for Hold. "Follow" and "Guard" are not official commands.

**[LOCKED] Abilities.** All five exist at the start: Veil Sight, Guardian Instinct, Veil Energy, Tracking Sense, Spirit Guidance. None is an automatic answer. Veil Sight does not reveal every clue, Spirit Guidance gives no direct answers or navigation, and Tracking Sense does not solve the mystery. Ember offers observations, warnings and possibilities that Asher must interpret.

| Ability | [SLICE] What it does | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Veil Sight | Ember fixates on or avoids certain spots, with a subtle cue | Never highlights every clue and can be wrong |
| Guardian Instinct | Steps between Asher and a perceived threat and warns by posture | Not an auto-attack; can raise false alarms |
| Veil Energy | Ember's behavior shifts near Veil-influenced places | Not a weapon or a resource meter |
| Tracking Sense | Follows a trail toward where someone went | Shows where, not why; the trail can be lost |
| Spirit Guidance | Rare authored moments of calm or hesitation | Gives no directions or answers; does not replace the Holy Spirit |

**[SLICE]** Ember uses local perception only. He is an animal, not a damage button, and the commands are available in a light form. He never acts as a worshipper, authority or symbol.

# 12. Evidence and confidence system

**[LOCKED]** Report labels are Observed, Reported, Inferred, Unconfirmed. Labels describe evidence strength, not truth. "Unverified" belongs to player-drawn Evidence Board connections.

**[SLICE] Mechanics.**

- **Two layers.** The Scout's labels are the historical record and never change retroactively. On site each claim also has a working status: Confirmed, Contradicted, or Still open.
- **What confirms a claim.** Direct observation or physical evidence. A Reported claim needs Asher's own observation or two independent sources. One frightened resident's word alone keeps it Reported.
- **Evidence capture.** Physical evidence can be photographed. Testimony is recorded as a note with its speaker and context.
- **Player connections.** Linking the visitor's relative to the overdue repairman is marked Unverified until something confirms it, such as a marked toolkit or the repairman's trail.
- **No oracle.** The board never reveals the answer and highlights a contradiction only when the player places two incompatible items together.
- **No reward.** Verifying grants no XP, Aces, Credits or items. The reward is a better decision.

# 13. Dorian Pike and Wren

**[LOCKED] Pike.** The local leader. Calm, reasonable and able to explain the situation. Not revealed as purely evil, and never a one-dimensional villain. His true condition stays uncertain until Asher gathers enough evidence. He may be compromised, trapped, protecting something, rationalizing harmful choices, or still capable of change.

**[LOCKED] Wren.** A frightened resident who wants out. She offers a human perspective with useful information, fear and uncertainty, and is not an exposition machine. The player judges both through investigation, not by being told.

**[SLICE] Details.**

- **Pike** is in his mid-fifties, a former coordinator of the outpost's supply and logistics. He is tired and persuasive because much of what he says is true. What he is classed as in BTV's enemy system is UNKNOWN.
- **How he is revealed.** A plausible first impression, then small contradictions, physical evidence, Wren's testimony, and his words under pressure, which differ with how Asher approaches him. Asher may leave without every layer.
- **Wren** is in her twenties, frightened and partly complicit through coercion, not malice. Her account mixes checkable fact, fear and gaps. She may contradict herself under stress and has her own reasons for not having left already.

# 14. Repairman investigation

**[LOCKED]** The repairman is missing and not confirmed dead. His fate is unresolved at the start. Presentation is never graphic. The investigation may establish that something happened to him, but the player never gets an instant, complete explanation. His fate may connect to the larger Veil and Devourer threat without naming or revealing the Devourers.

**[SLICE] Evidence.** Physical and non-graphic: belongings, a work log, a recorded stay that simply ends, signs of a hurried tidy-up. A gap remains for Asher to reason across. The slice ends with Asher able to say what he can and cannot conclude, and the Covenant's follow-up addresses the rest. A possible link to a larger threat appears only as an unexplained detail near the camp, consistent with the Veil's quiet.

# 15. Player response options

**[LOCKED]** The five options are Speak/Preach, Investigate, Rescue, Defend and Withdraw. Preaching never guarantees conversion. No option earns XP, Aces, Credits, equipment or a morality score.

| Option | [SLICE] What it looks like | Requirement | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speak/Preach | Honest, patient conversation, possibly Scripture | Earlier listening makes it possible | Some listen, some refuse, some change later |
| Investigate | Keep gathering and cross-checking before acting | Time, evidence, nerve | More information, more exposure to risk |
| Rescue | Get Wren and anyone else who wants out to safety, and follow any lead on the repairman | Locate people and plan a route | Costs time and tactical advantage, and may provoke the group |
| Defend | Fight only when attacked or when someone is in immediate danger | Light combat: attack, aim, dodge | Hostiles can retreat or yield (Enemy Morale); mercy is possible |
| Withdraw | Leave and report | None | People remain at risk; the Covenant acts on what Asher reports |

Options combine, and Withdraw is a legitimate choice, never a failure state.

# 17. Asher's final report

**[LOCKED]** Asher files his own final report.

**[SLICE]**

- **Structure.** It mirrors the Scout Report: area, what he observed, what was reported to him, what he inferred, what remains unconfirmed, and a recommendation.
- **No over-claiming.** The player chooses which conclusions to include, but the system limits the labels to what the evidence supports. A claim cannot be marked Observed unless Asher observed it.
- **Covenant reply.** A duty officer acknowledges it, asks hard questions ("How sure are you?"), and assigns follow-up from the outcome flags. The Covenant can disagree with Asher. It is organized but not infallible.
- **Result.** No promotion, rank change or reward. The slice ends with consequences and the option to return to Logos Rhema or continue.

# 19. Slice-scoped system stubs

All three are **SLICE-SCOPED STUB, NOT FINAL SYSTEM.** Each stub implements an interface in `core/interfaces/`. Slice code depends only on the interface, so the full system can later replace the stub without rebuilding the slice. None redefines the reserved architecture.

## Companions stub

- **Does:** Ember's presence, the seven commands, the five ability behaviors, state changes (for example Hold or Regroup), and interactions in this slice. Extends the preferred `CompanionBase`.
- **Interface:** `issue_command(command)`, `get_state()`, `get_ability_hints()`, plus signals `command_accepted` and `state_changed`.
- **Does not:** manage a party, bonding progression, needs, or any companion other than Ember.
- **Replacement:** the full companion system implements `CompanionBase`; Ember's scene keeps the same signals.

## Communication stub

- **Does:** delivers Covenant alerts, the Scout Report, Asher's outgoing report, relevant radio dialogue and mission messages, with context gating (nothing during a service phase or in the Communion zone).
- **Interface:** `send(message)`, `pending_for(context)`, `mark_read(id)`, signal `message_delivered`. Messages are resources with a type, source, body and urgency.
- **Does not:** simulate networks, smartphones, apps, unknown-number investigation, or signal dead zones beyond a per-zone `signal_ok` value.
- **Replacement:** the full communication system implements the same interface and adds the rest.

## World_states stub

- **Does:** stores flags (Section 16) and exposes them to later content.
- **Interface:** `set_flag(key, value)`, `get_flag(key, default)`, `snapshot()`, `restore(snapshot)`, signal `flag_changed`.
- **Does not:** simulate regions, propagate effects, schedule events, or compute any score.
- **Replacement:** the full system implements the same interface; keys stay namespaced.

```
    save_service.gd

  npc/                    # npc_base.gd, routine_runner.gd
  church/                 # service_director.gd, communion_zone.gd, offering.gd

  investigation/          # evidence_board.gd, claim.gd, verification_rules.gd
  dialogue/               # dialogue_runner.gd, listen_first.gd
  combat/                 # light_combat.gd, enemy_morale.gd
  veil/                   # zone_veil_state.gd
  world/
    logos_rhema/
    vehicle_ride/
    ridgeview/
  ui/                     # hud_contexts/, journal/
  data/
    services/  reports/  claims/  routines/  dialogue/   # .tres resources
  tests/
```

**Conventions.**

- **Typed GDScript** everywhere, with one class per file.
- **Interfaces before stubs.** Slice code depends on `core/interfaces/` only; stubs sit behind the autoload slots and can be swapped by changing one entry.
- **Data-driven content.** Service phases, reports, claims, NPC routines and dialogue are Resources in `data/`, never hard-coded.
- **Signals through EventBus** for cross-module events; modules do not reach into each other's nodes.
- **Movement and camera are separate** scripts.
- **Local perception** for NPCs, enemies and Ember; no global omniscience.
- **Android-first:** profile each phase on the device, cap simultaneous animated characters by device tier, and keep UI text-first.

**Needs your approval before use (not canon):** a test framework add-on such as GUT or gdUnit4 (an external dependency), and the choice between Godot's Mobile and Compatibility renderers, to be settled after the Phase 0 device profile.
