BEYOND THE VEIL:
THE LAST WATCH
MASTER GAME & CHARACTER BIBLE
Current Established Version
Protagonist: Asher Vance • Organization: The Covenant • Genre: Christian Action-Adventure /
Survival
This document consolidates the concepts currently established for Beyond the Veil: The Last 
Watch. Items not explicitly established remain open for later decisions.
1. Core Game Identity
Create a cinematic, action-focused Christian modern action-adventure survival game. It is fictional 
and original while drawing inspiration from Christian faith, biblical themes, spiritual warfare, the 
Armor of God, Scripture, prayer, evangelism, redemption and end-times themes.
Do not simply retell the Old Testament or New Testament. The story takes place in a modern world 
where supernatural events connected to an approaching end-times crisis begin affecting humanity.
Core experience: Action + Adventure + Survival + Exploration + Leadership + Faith + Story + 
Relationships + Community.
2. The World & Semi-Open Structure
The world is semi-open rather than fully open. It contains several large interconnected regions that
can be explored, revisited and gradually unlocked.
Regions may include modern cities, settlements, wilderness, abandoned facilities, dangerous 
districts, hidden locations and areas where supernatural influence is increasingly strong.
Exploration is important, but the game retains a strong main storyline.
3. The Veil
The Veil is the mysterious boundary between the ordinary modern world and supernatural reality.
At first, supernatural incidents are isolated and difficult to understand. As the story progresses, the 
Covenant discovers connections between these incidents and a much larger end-times conflict.
The weakening of the Veil is a major long-term story mystery.

4. The Covenant
The Covenant is a hidden Christian organization. Asher Vance is already a member when the game
begins.
The Covenant protects people, investigates supernatural events, resists evil, rescues communities, 
spreads the Gospel and prepares for the growing threat around the Veil.
It is not a government agency and does not replace or represent the Church as a whole.
5. Covenant Hierarchy & Branches
Hierarchy: Recruits Agents/Specialists Team Leaders Commanders Generals. → → → →
Asher begins as an experienced field member, later becomes a Team Leader, then a Commander, 
and eventually a General. Promotions are earned through story progression, successful missions, 
leadership, service and responsibility rather than ordinary XP alone.
Specialized roles include Guardians (combat/protection), Messengers (communication/evangelism),
Watchers (investigation/intelligence), Shepherds (rescue/support/restoration), Scouts 
(reconnaissance/enemy detection), and Keepers (equipment/resources).
Rank represents responsibility and organizational authority, not spiritual superiority.
6. Protagonist — Asher Vance
Established name: Asher Vance.
Asher is already a trained Covenant field member at the beginning. He does not start as a General.
His progression is field member Team Leader Commander General. Increasing rank → → →
expands his leadership and operational responsibilities.
Asher can fight supernatural enemies, use the Armor of God, wield the Sword of the Spirit, pray, 
receive spiritual guidance, work with teammates, give tactical advice, participate in evangelism 
and community restoration, and lead major Covenant operations.
Further personal details such as exact age, appearance, family and hometown remain open unless 
separately decided.
7. God, Prayer & the Holy Spirit
God is the ultimate authority and source of Asher's spiritual mission. God is not a normal playable 
combat character or controllable teammate.

God's presence can be represented through prayer, guidance, wisdom, timing, signs, blessings and 
story events.
Prayer can occur before, during or after important situations and can seek guidance, strength, 
wisdom, protection, help, blessing or knowledge. Results are not guaranteed to be immediate; 
divine timing and God's will remain part of the game's spiritual theme.
The Holy Spirit can be represented through guidance, conviction, warnings, wisdom, direction, 
spiritual discernment and moments of clarity. It should not simply be a controllable attack button.
8. Scripture & Battle Declarations
Asher can occasionally recite or declare Scripture during important battles. References can include
Philippians 4:13, Psalm 23, Psalm 91, Romans 8:31 and passages connected to the Armor of God.
For a particularly important boss fight, Asher can begin reciting Psalm 23 while actively fighting, 
continue the recitation through the battle and finish it as the boss is defeated. This should be a 
rare, cinematic gameplay moment.
Scripture represents faith, courage, prayer and reliance on God rather than functioning as a simple
magical spell.
Established Covenant battle vow: “By truth we stand. By faith we endure. By the Light we rise. 
Darkness will not prevail.” Asher can eventually lead the team in saying it before major battles.
9. Armor of God
Asher progressively obtains and equips: Belt of Truth; Breastplate of Righteousness; Shoes of the 
Gospel of Peace; Shield of Faith; Helmet of Salvation; Sword of the Spirit.
Pieces are acquired progressively and have meaningful gameplay functions rather than being 
purely cosmetic.
10. Enemies
Destroyers: combat-focused supernatural enemies with physical strength, powerful movement, 
close-range attacks and supernatural abilities.
Persuaders: enemies that influence, deceive, manipulate or persuade people, creating community 
and character-focused situations.
Deceivers: intelligent enemies specializing in illusions, manipulation, ambushes, misinformation 
and hidden operations.
Stronger enemy commanders and major bosses exist above ordinary enemies. Enemy forces can 
patrol, move, gather and conduct operations rather than simply waiting for the player.

11. Scouts & Dynamic Enemy Activity
Covenant scouts travel through the world, gather intelligence and report unusual enemy activity.
Reports can reveal concentrations of Destroyers or other threats and trigger occasional organized 
battles or operations.
The player may choose to engage, investigate, rescue civilians, gather information, send another 
team, or continue another mission.
Combat should not happen constantly; the world should feel alive through moving enemy groups, 
patrols, communities and changing situations.
12. Combat
Combat combines melee combat, supernatural abilities, equipment, Armor abilities, pet 
cooperation, teammate cooperation and tactical decisions.
There are ordinary encounters and occasional major battles. Boss encounters should have distinct 
mechanics, arenas, identities and tactical requirements.
13. Currency & Enemy Drops
The fictional in-game currency is Covenant Credits.
Credits can be earned through missions, exploration, helping communities, recovering resources 
and operations. They can support equipment, crafting resources, transportation, safehouse 
improvements and other appropriate systems.
Faith itself is never something the player purchases.
Defeated enemies can leave resources, clues, materials, artifacts or equipment-related items. Rare 
enemies can provide rarer drops.
14. Teammates & Leadership
Covenant teammates have individual identities, personalities, skills, roles, histories, strengths and 
weaknesses.
They can assist with combat, exploration, rescue and investigation. Trust and relationships can 
develop through the player's actions.
Teammates may reward Asher for helping them with weapons, equipment, upgrade materials, 
information, special missions or other useful resources.

During selected missions, teammates may ask Asher for advice. Possible instructions include hold 
position, push forward, scout first, protect civilians, retreat and protect a teammate. Advice 
influences AI behavior.
Leadership grows with Asher's rank. Later, he can make decisions affecting multiple teams or 
regions.
15. Evangelism, Rescue & Restoration
The game is not purely about fighting enemies. Missions can involve talking with people, helping 
communities, rescuing civilians, investigating deception, protecting vulnerable people, restoring 
damaged communities and sharing Christian teachings through appropriate dialogue.
Helping people has consequences. A person helped earlier may later become an ally, provide 
information, offer resources or help another community.
Winning does not always mean defeating an enemy physically; it can mean rescuing someone, 
exposing deception, protecting a community, making a wise decision or preserving hope.
16. The Pet — Core Character Bible
Asher has an animal companion. It initially appears relatively normal, while its unusual abilities 
are revealed progressively.
Pet growth combines three systems: Physical Growth + Bond Growth + Spiritual Awakening.
Physical growth can increase strength, speed, durability, jumping, endurance and tracking. 
Established physical traits include powerful jaws and powerful claws.
Bond growth improves cooperation, commands, special attacks and emergency behaviors as the 
relationship develops.
Spiritual awakening can reveal Veil Sight, Guardian Instinct, Veil Energy, supernatural detection 
and special protective abilities.
The pet's full potential must not be revealed at the beginning.
17. Pet Powers
Veil Sight: detects supernatural entities, hidden enemies, supernatural traces, concealed paths and 
disturbances in the Veil.
Predator Physiology: powerful jaws and claws, enhanced speed, reflexes, jumping, tracking and 
combat strength.
Guardian Instinct: instinctively protects Asher or a teammate when serious danger is detected.

Veil Energy: eventually channels supernatural energy through attacks.
Tracking Sense: tracks enemies, missing people, Covenant members and supernatural traces.
Spirit Guidance: at rare moments, reacts to something before Asher understands what is 
happening, potentially leading toward danger, safety, hidden areas or people needing help.
18. Pet Commands & Adaptive AI
The pet uses tactical commands plus adaptive AI.
Commands include Attack, Protect, Scout, Track, Retrieve, Hold Position and Regroup.
The pet should behave like a genuine companion, not a simple programmable weapon.
It can intelligently react to changing circumstances. For example, it may interrupt an attack to 
protect Asher from a sudden threat, then return to its original objective.
As the bond grows, the pet becomes better at anticipating Asher's needs.
19. Survival & Exploration
Survival supports the adventure without overwhelming it. Potential systems include resources, 
equipment maintenance, safe locations, support items and environmental hazards.
Exploration can reveal hidden Covenant locations, enemy activity, resources, lore, secrets, people, 
supernatural traces and optional missions.
Exact survival mechanics remain open for later optimization.
20. Safehouses & Operations
Covenant safehouses can provide equipment, rest, mission planning, team management, crafting, 
pet interaction, story conversations and scout reports.
Major operations can follow: Scout report Investigation Team selection Preparation → → → →
Travel Combat/Rescue Objective Consequences Rewards. → → → →
21. Story Progression
Early game: Asher is an established Covenant field member. The player learns the world, Covenant,
combat, exploration, pet, enemies, prayer and basic spiritual systems.
Early-mid game: Asher earns greater responsibility and becomes a Team Leader. Enemy activity 
becomes more organized and the Veil mystery deepens.

Mid game: Covenant investigations reveal larger connections. Asher's leadership grows, the pet's 
deeper abilities emerge, and the Armor of God becomes increasingly important.
Late game: Asher becomes Commander and eventually General. He can influence major operations
while the Covenant prepares for The Last Watch.
Endgame: The Last Watch becomes the central crisis. Asher's faith, leadership, combat ability, 
relationships, Covenant responsibilities, pet bond and knowledge of the Veil all become important.
22. The Last Watch
The Last Watch is the Covenant's critical period of preparation and action as the supernatural 
conflict reaches an unprecedented level.
Beyond the Veil means discovering what exists beyond ordinary human perception. The Last 
Watch means the Covenant's final great period of vigilance and responsibility.
23. End-Times Connection
The game draws inspiration from biblical end-times themes while remaining an original fictional 
interpretation.
Do not simply copy biblical events and turn them into missions. Instead, gradually reveal 
connections between the supernatural conflict and an approaching end-times crisis.
24. Core Gameplay Loop
Explore Discover Investigate Scout Choose Fight / Rescue / Help Collect Upgrade → → → → → → → →
Build Relationships Lead Explore Further. → →
The player should sometimes fight, sometimes investigate, sometimes rescue, sometimes 
evangelize, sometimes lead, and sometimes pray or wait.
25. Core Creative Philosophy
Make Asher powerful, but not the ultimate source of power.
Make combat exciting, but do not make violence the entire purpose.
Make Scripture meaningful rather than decorative.
Make prayer meaningful without guaranteeing instant supernatural rewards.
Make the Covenant feel organized and alive.
Make teammates feel like actual people rather than disposable NPCs.

Make the pet feel like a genuine companion whose full potential is discovered over time.
Make the world feel alive through scouts, enemy movements, communities, missions and changing
situations.
The central feeling should be: a servant becoming a leader, not a person becoming a god.
