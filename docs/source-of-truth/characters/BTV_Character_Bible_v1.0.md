# BEYOND THE VEIL: THE LAST WATCH
# BTV CHARACTER BIBLE v1.0

**Document Status:** SPECIALIZED REFERENCE — CHARACTER AUTHORITY  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. AUTHORITY & PURPOSE
This Character Bible is subordinate to:
1. Explicit Creator canon decisions
2. Latest Creator corrections
3. BTV Master Canon
4. BTV Story & Lore Bible v1.0

It governs character-specific details only within those boundaries. UNKNOWN and PROPOSED material must never silently become canon.

## 2. CHARACTER DESIGN PRINCIPLE
BTV characters are people before they are gameplay functions. Characters should have identity, agency, motivations, limitations, relationships, responsibilities, strengths, weaknesses, and room for growth. Character development should support faith, truth, service, courage, redemption, community, leadership, discernment, perseverance, and responsibility.

## 3. CHARACTER STATUS SYSTEM
**CANON:** Established by the Creator or higher-authority BTV material.  
**CANON DIRECTION:** Established direction with implementation/details open.  
**PROPOSED:** Idea for consideration; not canon.  
**UNKNOWN:** Not established; do not invent.  
**CONFLICT:** Contradicts higher authority and must be rejected or escalated.

## 4. ASHER VANCE
**Name:** Asher Vance  
**Age:** 16–19  
**Status:** CANON  
**Role:** Chosen / Covenant-trained field member  
**Leadership trajectory:** Field role → Team Leader → Commander → General

Asher's progression is based on responsibility, service, trust, leadership, and decisions rather than ordinary kill/XP progression.

Asher must not be portrayed as divine, spiritually infallible, automatically superior, the source of God's authority, a replacement for Christ, or a replacement for the Church.

**UNKNOWN:** exact age, appearance, birthplace, family history, detailed biography, personality, hobbies, fears, weaknesses, early Covenant history, exact Chosen-selection circumstances, relationship to the dual-Chosen concept, detailed combat style, and detailed dialogue voice.

## 5. THE CHOSEN
The Chosen is human, not divine, and does not replace Christ.

The older selectable Male/Female Chosen concept remains **UNKNOWN — CANON DECISION REQUIRED**. Do not invent a second Chosen's name, biography, appearance, personality, selection mechanics, relationship to Asher, or whether both exist simultaneously.

## 6. COVENANT CHARACTERIZATION
The Covenant is a hidden organization serving God and protecting people. It is not the Church as a whole, a government agency, or a hierarchy of spiritual worth.

Hierarchy:
- Recruits
- Agents / Specialists
- Team Leaders
- Commanders
- Generals

Rank represents responsibility and trust, not closeness to God.

## 7. COVENANT BRANCHES
**Guardians:** combat/protection — courage, discipline, restraint, responsibility.  
**Messengers:** communication/evangelism — communication, listening, truth, outreach, discernment.  
**Watchers:** investigation/intelligence — observation, evidence, patience, analysis, caution.  
**Shepherds:** rescue/support/restoration — care, rescue, patience, recovery, community.  
**Scouts:** reconnaissance — awareness, mobility, observation, adaptability, reporting.  
**Keepers:** equipment/resources — preparation, logistics, stewardship, maintenance.

No branch is morally superior to another.

## 8. COVENANT OATH
“By truth we stand. / By faith we endure. / By the Light we rise. / Darkness will not prevail.”

Use meaningfully rather than constantly.

## 9. TEAMMATES
Teammates are characters first and gameplay companions second. They should have distinct roles, personalities, strengths, weaknesses, relationships, opinions, judgment, and histories once established.

Named teammates are currently **UNKNOWN**. Do not invent permanent names, biographies, appearances, or backstories as canon.

## 10. TEAMMATE RELATIONSHIPS
Relationships may affect trust, communication, tactical coordination, information, special missions, equipment opportunities, emotional reactions, leadership situations, recovery, and willingness to follow commands.

No numerical reputation system is currently canon.

## 11. LEADERSHIP RELATIONSHIPS
Asher's progression should change responsibility, trust, disagreement, delegation, accountability, and consequences. Promotion does not mean automatic perfection.

## 12. EMBER
Ember is an established companion, not merely a weapon. Roles include companionship, tracking, protection, exploration, Veil-related perception, adaptive assistance, and spiritual/narrative significance.

Established abilities:
1. Veil Sight
2. Guardian Instinct
3. Veil Energy
4. Tracking Sense
5. Spirit Guidance

Development dimensions:
- physical growth
- behavioral development
- bond development
- spiritual awakening

## 13. EMBER COMMANDS
**CANON-DIRECTION / CONFIRMATION REQUIRED**

Attack, Protect, Scout, Track, Retrieve, Hold, Regroup.

Implementation remains open. Species, origin, exact history, full nature, and ultimate significance remain **UNKNOWN**. Ember does not replace the Holy Spirit.

## 14. CHARACTER ARC PRINCIPLE
A character arc should change understanding, behavior, relationships, responsibility, or outlook. It should not exist only to unlock a weapon, stat, cosmetic, or quest reward.

## 15. REDEMPTION
Redemption is a major BTV theme. It may involve wrongdoing/failure, recognition, accountability, consequences, repentance or meaningful change, and restoration where appropriate.

Redemption does not erase consequences. Forgiveness, trust, reconciliation, and restoration are not automatically identical.

## 16. CHARACTER CONSEQUENCES
Character decisions may affect personal life, teammates, communities, Covenant operations, and future narrative situations. BTV has no universal morality meter.

## 17. ANTAGONIST CHARACTER PRINCIPLE
Major antagonists should have objectives, methods, relationships, influence, internal logic, consequences, and narrative significance. Their complete origins and hierarchy remain open unless canonized.

## 18. ENEMY CLASSES
**Destroyers:** direct aggression and physical threat.  
**Persuaders:** influence, manipulation, temptation, or corruption through people and communities.  
**Deceivers:** illusions, misinformation, false perception, ambushes, and dangerous manipulation of information.  
**Commanders/Bosses:** coordinate threats and have greater narrative significance.

Classification describes story/game function, not complete origin.

## 19. HUMAN ANTAGONISM
BTV may involve ordinary people, institutions, corrupted leadership, social pressure, manipulation, injustice, misunderstanding, and competing interests.

Do not equate disagreement with evil, poverty with moral failure, wealth with righteousness, or mistakes with permanent condemnation.

No specific human antagonist is currently canonized.

## 20. CHRISTIAN CHARACTERIZATION
Characters may experience doubt, fear, uncertainty, grief, temptation, failure, courage, hope, responsibility, forgiveness, service, and discernment. Faith does not make characters emotionally flat or permanently fearless.

## 21. GOD, CHRIST & HOLY SPIRIT
God is the ultimate authority. Christ remains above the Covenant and the Chosen. The Holy Spirit may be represented through guidance, conviction, warnings, wisdom, and discernment, but is not a controllable combat ability or resource wheel.

## 22. PRAYER
Prayer can reveal gratitude, fear, repentance, uncertainty, dependence, hope, grief, requests for wisdom, and concern for others. Prayer is not a guaranteed transaction and should not exist merely as a mechanical buff.

## 23. SCRIPTURE
Scripture should be accurate, contextual, purposeful, and restrained. Specific cinematic quotations remain subject to their existing canon status. Never invent or misrepresent biblical passages.

## 24. CHURCH & COMMUNITY CHARACTERS
Church characters should feel like ordinary people with different responsibilities, personalities, ages, occupations, concerns, gifts, struggles, and relationships. Churches are living communities, not merely quest hubs.

## 25. HCI
Harvest Chapel International is established as real-world inspiration. The exact degree of direct representation remains **UNKNOWN — CANON DECISION REQUIRED**. Do not invent official in-game identities or institutional representation.

## 26. KITFIT & SERVICE
Church service is a living community activity. KITFIT is an established pre-preaching segment involving Scripture and a short message, including teaching about giving. Offerings are voluntary. Wealth is not a measure of spiritual worth. KITFIT's acronym expansion remains **UNKNOWN**.

## 27. COMMUNION
The Communion Set consists of bread and wine as one established item. Communion occurs inside the CommunionContextZone. First Sunday Communion is established. Communion is reflective/contextual and provides no buffs, XP, health restoration, combat statistics, or supernatural purchase mechanics.

## 28. NPC CHARACTER PRINCIPLE
NPCs should feel like inhabitants rather than decoration. They may work, worship, trade, travel, rest, raise families, help, investigate, shelter, flee, organize, disagree, and cooperate.

## 29. POPULATION COMPOSITION
Public/community spaces should contain substantially more civilians, families, workers, visitors, worshippers, and merchants than Covenant agents. Covenant bases may naturally contain more Covenant personnel. Composition varies by location, district, settlement, facility, chapel, wilderness, and crisis level.

## 30. VISUAL CONTINUITY
Track age, build, face, hair, clothing, equipment, branch, rank, environment, and justified injuries/scars where appropriate. Visual changes should have narrative or practical reasons.

## 31. COVENANT UNIFORM
Established direction: white base, restrained gold, cross-centered Christian insignia. Variants: Modern Tactical, Formal/Ceremonial, Field/Stealth, Regional/Weather, Specialist. Variation can reflect branch, rank, duty, and environment. Do not turn uniforms into random fashion loot.

## 32. DIALOGUE
Dialogue should reveal character, situation, relationship, knowledge, emotion, intention, and consequence. Avoid making every character sound identical.

## 33. CHARACTER VOICE
Once a named character is canonized, maintain a voice profile covering speech rhythm, vocabulary, emotional tendencies, humor, seriousness, communication habits, conflict style, faith language where appropriate, and verbal patterns.

## 34. KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARIES
Characters should know only what they plausibly know. They do not automatically know Veil truths, Covenant secrets, Asher's private decisions, hidden enemy plans, future events, or information learned elsewhere.

## 35. CHARACTER RECORD TEMPLATE
Future named characters should track: name, status, age, role, appearance, personality, background, motivations, strengths, weaknesses, relationships, faith/worldview characterization where relevant, skills, narrative function, arc, key events, dialogue profile, gameplay role, equipment, current state, unknowns, proposed concepts, and continuity notes.

## 36. CHANGE MANAGEMENT
Significant character changes should record:
**Previous:** what was established.  
**New:** what the Creator establishes.  
**Impact:** story/gameplay/relationship/visual/technical consequences.  
**CANON UPDATED:** confirmation of authority.

AI must not silently overwrite character canon.

**Canon → Character Concept → Character Function → Personality/Behavior → Relationships → Arc → Dialogue → Gameplay Role → Visual Design → Implementation → Testing → Canon Audit → Documentation**

## 41. BTV CHARACTER TEST
A strong BTV character should feel human, have agency, have meaningful strengths and limitations, contribute something unique, fit the Christian worldview without becoming a stereotype, interact naturally with society, have believable relationships and consequences, support the story rather than merely explain lore, and remain meaningful without a gameplay reward.

## 42. AI CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT RULES
AI must distinguish established canon, proposed material, unknown information, and Creator decisions required. If a missing detail materially affects a character, flag it rather than quietly deciding it.
