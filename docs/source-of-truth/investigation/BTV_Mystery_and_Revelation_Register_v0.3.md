## 1. Authority and status rules

Use the current project authority order:
1. Creator's latest explicit instruction or correction.
2. Master Canon as reconciled by accepted creator decisions.
3. Specialized Bibles within their domains.
4. Vertical Slice Specification for its locked slice details.
5. Older handoffs, mockups and generated material.

This register uses the user's preferred working labels:
- **CONFIRMED:** directly established by a higher-authority source.
- **PROPOSED:** design option, not canon unless separately approved.
- **UNKNOWN:** intentionally unresolved or not established.
- **BLOCKED:** cannot safely be designed or implemented until a dependency is resolved.
- **COMPLETED:** the specific work item is finished; this does not mean the mystery itself is resolved.

Where an older document says “unknown” but a later accepted decision establishes a story direction, this register records the later direction and preserves any remaining unknowns.

## 4. Five-revelation pacing register

This is the proposed narrative learning structure, not the six incident-stage classification.

1. **The Signs:** irregular observations appear; their causes remain open.
2. **The Pattern:** independent clues begin to connect; ordinary alternatives are still tested.
3. **The Influence:** the player sees meaningful effects on people, information or environments.
4. **The Competing Understandings:** characters interpret the evidence differently; their knowledge and incentives are inspectable.
5. **The Arc Revelation:** the local promise is answered with defensible evidence, while larger questions remain deliberately open.

The six incident states remain separate: Normal → Disturbed → Influenced → Veil-Touched → Veil Breach → Critical/Endgame-Level. They are not a one-to-one mapping to the five revelations.

## 5. Concordance continuity controls

Because the accepted story direction introduces Marek Sloane and the Concordance, future writers must preserve these constraints:

- Sloane is human; not Antichrist, Satan, God or a replacement for a final supernatural explanation.
- The Concordance manipulates information, exploits institutional weaknesses, pressures witnesses and takes advantage of Veil incidents; it does not create or command the Veil.
- Its members are not morally identical. Some are opportunists, some misled or coerced, and some may withdraw or make amends.
- Do not connect every local crisis to the Concordance. Each connection must be supported by a traceable evidence chain.
- The Ridgeview slice does not identify the Devourers or the Concordance to the player unless a later explicit creator decision changes the locked slice.

## 6. Mystery acceptance checklist

Before a mystery enters a mission or story outline, verify:
- The player-facing question is clear.
- The story knows whether it intends to answer the question, answer it partially or leave it open.
- The answer is known to writers before final clue scripting, unless deliberate uncertainty is the actual point.
- Each clue has provenance and a plausible source.
- Character knowledge is bounded and traceable.
- Red herrings have fair explanations.
- The reveal does not contradict a higher-authority source.
- The reveal does not rely on a newly invented power or rule introduced after the clue.
- The mystery is not solved by prayer, Scripture, the Holy Spirit or Ember as an oracle.
- The payoff is not withheld indefinitely after being clearly promised.
- The consequence of discovery affects people, choices or the world in a coherent way.
- Any unresolved follow-on question is recorded explicitly.
