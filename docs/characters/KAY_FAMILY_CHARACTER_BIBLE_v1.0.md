**CONFIRMED FOUNDATION**
- Major independent character.
- Equal narrative importance to Steph Kay.
- Characterization combines a fictionalized version of the creator's personality with original fictional traits.
- Must have independent motivations, decisions, relationships and character progression.

**OPEN**
- Exact age, appearance, biography, Covenant status, branch, rank, skills, ambitions and detailed personality.

**PROPOSED STORY PRINCIPLE**
Adom can challenge Asher's reasoning and contribute perspectives that Asher may overlook. His value should come from judgement, choices and demonstrated capability, not from being related to the creator.

### 3.2 Steph Kay

**CONFIRMED FOUNDATION**
- Major independent character.
- Equal narrative importance to Adom.
- Has his own storyline and identity; not merely a companion or sidekick.
- Has a strong interest and skill in fashion, especially contemporary and trendy styling, while maintaining a Christian lifestyle.

**CONFIRMED FASHION DIRECTION — TRENDY CHRISTIAN STREETWEAR**
- Understands current trends and how to put outfits together.
- Can style coordinated casual and streetwear looks, including appropriate footwear and accessories.
- His style can be expressive, youthful and confident while remaining consistent with his Christian values.
- Do not reduce his personality to fashion or imply that expensive brands are necessary for style.

**OPEN**
- Exact age, appearance, biography, Covenant status, branch, rank, ambitions and detailed personality.

### 3.4 Ellen Dewealth

**CONFIRMED FOUNDATION**
- Mother of Adom and Steph.
- Chartered Accountant in real life.
- Important character in BTV.
- Covenant member.
- Her close relationship with God is a defining part of her character.

**CONFIRMED FASHION DIRECTION — OCCASION-AWARE ELEGANCE**
- Has a strong sense of what is appropriate to wear for different occasions.
- Understands casual, official, formal, church and social-event dressing.
- Coordinates clothing, footwear and accessories thoughtfully.
- Her style should communicate discernment, elegance and confidence without making expensive clothing a measure of worth.

Her faith should be shown through a sincere personal relationship with God, prayer, Scripture, humility, compassion, wisdom, integrity and daily conduct. Her faith is not a numerical stat, a purchasable advantage or a claim that every personal opinion is a divine instruction. She does not replace the Holy Spirit or become infallible.

**PROPOSED PROFESSIONAL STORY FUNCTION**

**OPEN**

## 4. Shared family fashion identity

**CONFIRMED CREATOR DIRECTION:** Everyone in the family knows how to dress. Their styles are distinct rather than interchangeable.

| Character | Signature direction | Wardrobe range |
|---|---|---|
| Adom Kay | Versatile, between different styles | Casual, smart-casual, trendy and official |
| Steph Kay | Trend-aware Christian streetwear | Contemporary casual, streetwear and occasion-appropriate looks |
| Stephen Kay | Professional versatility | Business/formal, professional casual and smart-casual |
| Ellen Dewealth | Occasion-aware elegance | Casual, official, formal, church and social occasions |

### Production rules — proposed implementation standards

1. Clothing must fit the scene, weather, activity, location and character role.
2. Each character needs a recognizable style identity across multiple outfits.
3. Christian values should inform the family's presentation without imposing one identical clothing formula on everyone.
4. Avoid treating modesty, faith, dignity or personal worth as a price tag or fashion score.
5. Do not make characters wear Covenant uniforms in ordinary civilian scenes unless the story context requires it.
6. Maintain continuity: record outfit changes by scene, time and location.
7. Keep designs age-appropriate and consistent with each character's approved age and reference.
8. No exact face, body, skin details, hairstyle, measurements, accessories or final costume design is canon until approved reference material exists.

## 5. Relationships and story integration

### Family relationships

**CONFIRMED:** Adom and Steph are brothers and both are major independent characters. Stephen and Ellen are their parents. All four are important to BTV.

**PROPOSED:** Show affection, disagreement, encouragement, humour, responsibility and reconciliation through ordinary family interactions. Avoid making the family unrealistically perfect or making one sibling dependent on the other.

### Relationship with Asher Vance

**PROPOSED, NOT LOCKED:**
- Adom and Asher may develop a close alliance through shared work, disagreement, evidence and mutual respect.
- Steph should meet and relate to Asher independently of Adom.
- Stephen may advise Asher on professional accountability when the subject falls within his established expertise.
- Ellen may offer Christian encouragement and thoughtful counsel while respecting Asher's own responsibility and relationship with God.

These connections must develop naturally. Do not make all four family members automatically subordinate to Asher or assign them command authority without a canon decision.
