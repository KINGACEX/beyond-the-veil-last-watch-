## Ellen's display name

- **Ellen DW** is her standard display name throughout ordinary BTV content: dialogue, UI, mission records, character lists and public-facing documentation.
- **Dewealth** is a deliberate, occasional Easter egg reserved for two contextual settings:
  1. **Family-house scenes**, especially natural family conversations.
  2. **Church-event scenes**, including church festivals, celebrations or award ceremonies where her full family name could naturally be spoken or requested.
- If someone asks her name or addresses her by her full family name in either setting, she may say or reveal **“Dewealth”**.
- At a church event, the reveal may occur naturally when she is introduced, recognized, or receives an award under that name. Do not force the reveal into every event or assume that she wins an award.
- The reveal should feel like a subtle, memorable detail—not a repeated nickname, forced joke or routine label.
- Outside those specific contexts, use **Ellen DW**. Do not casually expand DW to Dewealth in ordinary content.

## Event and canon safeguards

- Church festivals, community celebrations and award events can provide plausible contexts for this Easter egg.
- The naming rule does not itself establish a particular festival, award title, award recipient, ceremony schedule or church programme as canon.
- Any specific event details must follow separately approved BTV world and church-event canon.
- Preserve the established Christian direction: awards should not imply spiritual superiority, purchased favour, or a measure of closeness to God.

## Continuity note

This addendum supersedes the ordinary-use naming references to **Ellen Dewealth** in the v1.0 character bible. Her established character foundations remain unchanged. The Easter egg is a naming detail, not a new biography or story arc.
