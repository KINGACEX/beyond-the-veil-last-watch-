## Ellen's display name

- **Ellen DW** is her standard display name throughout ordinary BTV content: dialogue, UI, mission records, character lists and public-facing documentation.
- **Dewealth** is deliberately reserved as a hidden family-house Easter egg.
- If someone asks her name or addresses her by her full family name in the family-house setting, she may say or reveal **“Dewealth”**.
- The reveal should feel like a natural, occasional detail in family dialogue—not a repeated nickname, forced joke or routine label.
- Outside that specific Easter-egg context, use **Ellen DW**. Do not casually expand DW to Dewealth in ordinary content.

## Continuity note

This addendum supersedes the ordinary-use naming references to **Ellen Dewealth** in the v1.0 character bible. Her established character foundations remain unchanged. The Easter egg is a naming detail, not a new biography or story arc.
