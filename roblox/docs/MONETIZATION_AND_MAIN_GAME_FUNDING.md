# BTV Roblox Monetization & Main-Game Funding Plan

**Status:** Proposed business plan; no products created, priced, or published.  
**Goal:** Support sustainable Roblox development and, if eligible earnings are converted through Roblox's official process, contribute to the original Godot BTV production budget.  
**Policy:** Free-first, fair and transparent: cosmetics plus optional quality-of-life convenience, never pay-to-win.

## 1. Monetization principles

1. The core game loop and opening story must be enjoyable without purchases.
2. Purchases must be optional, clearly described, and provide the advertised value.
3. No paid spiritual status, prayer, Communion, Scripture, divine favour, salvation, Holy Spirit interaction, Armor of God power, or faith-related advantage.
4. No pay-to-win combat/progression, paid mission completion, paid rank, paid Covenant authority, or paid access to story-critical evidence.
5. No randomized paid rewards, loot boxes, paid spins, gambling-like mechanics, misleading scarcity, countdown pressure, or manipulative purchase prompts.
6. Do not sell Aces or Covenant Credits in the first monetization release. Keep both currencies distinct and earnable through approved gameplay.
7. All premium purchases must be server-validated and granted reliably. Use Roblox's documented receipt handling for developer products.
8. The game must remain accessible to players who never spend Robux.

## 2. Proposed product catalogue

### Tier 1 — Cosmetic launch products

| Product | Roblox product type | Benefit | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asher Vance — Signature Appearance | One-time Game Pass | Cosmetic appearance only | Proposed |
| Covenant Casual Outfit Collection | Game Pass or separate avatar items | Approved outfit looks only | Proposed |
| Nameplate & Profile Presentation Pack | Game Pass | Visual nameplate/profile styling | Proposed |
| Emote & Gesture Collection | Game Pass | Non-combat expressive animations | Proposed |
| Personal Quarters Decor Set | Game Pass | Optional room decoration, no gameplay advantage | Proposed |

### Tier 2 — Collectible presentation

"Important artifacts" should be divided into story-critical canon artifacts and optional cosmetic collectibles.

- Story-critical artifacts, evidence, Scripture-related objects, Armor of God elements, Communion items and mission necessities must never be sold for Robux.
- Optional display replicas, journal covers, non-canonical decorative keepsakes and cosmetic collection showcases may be sold only after lore review.
- A paid decorative replica must not be represented as the sacred/original artifact or grant its narrative authority.
- No artifact may grant spiritual power, guaranteed guidance, prayer outcomes, divine favour or combat superiority.

### Tier 3 — Later, only after retention and value are proven

| Product | Type | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Private server option | Roblox private server setting | Public/default play remains accessible |
| Optional monthly supporter membership | Roblox subscription | Only if recurring benefits can be delivered every month; no gameplay advantage |
| Repeatable cosmetic bundle | Developer Product | Must grant a defined cosmetic item; receipt processing must be idempotent |

Do not launch subscriptions in v0.1/v0.2. Auto-renewing products create a continuing delivery obligation and require clear cancellation/value communication.


## 2A. Ember premium companion offer — proposed

Ember should have a premium product line, but **Ember must not become a paid requirement for completing the main story**. Ember is a meaningful companion and field partner in BTV canon. The free experience must retain a complete, fair route through every mission, including any mission that uses companion support.

### Proposed product structure

| Product | Type | What the player receives | Fairness boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ember — Companion Bond Pack | One-time Game Pass | Premium companion presentation, expanded bonding interactions, approved cosmetic accessories and a dedicated companion profile presentation | Does not gate the base Ember story role or required mission completion |
| Ember — Field Style Collection | Game Pass or separate cosmetic items | Alternate idle poses, recall animation, command animations, non-canonical visual trail effects and approved accessory looks | Visual/expression only; no stronger stats |
| Companion Ability Convenience Pack(s) | One-time Game Pass per companion or clearly priced bundle | Optional command presets, quick-command layouts, saved non-combat routines and companion-specific UX shortcuts | Reduces interface friction only; never changes world stats, mission outcomes, evidence access or competitive balance |
| Ember — Camp & Companion Interactions | Game Pass | Optional non-combat interactions, rest/camp animations and photo poses | No resource generation, healing advantage or progression boost |

### Ability policy

The seven approved commands remain part of the designed companion system: Attack, Protect, Scout, Track, Retrieve, Hold and Regroup. Do not sell access to the basic commands if a mission or tutorial expects players to use them. Keep the actual functional abilities and any balance-relevant upgrades earnable through ordinary gameplay for every player.

Premium may offer **companion-specific convenience abilities**: quicker command access, saved command presets, optional non-combat routines, contextual command shortcuts and clearer companion status/feedback. These can make interactions feel faster and the interface more efficient, but must not make the companion physically faster or stronger, improve detection or success probability, reveal paid-only evidence, or change mission outcomes. Free players must retain the same core command capability and the same quality of mission experience; their route may use a few more deliberate UI steps, but must remain reasonable and never frustrating by design.

### Ember product guardrails

- Ember is not a weapon, damage multiplier, loot generator, paid tracker that reveals otherwise hidden mission evidence, or shortcut around investigation.
- Do not sell enhanced damage, attack speed, health, detection radius, movement speed, guaranteed retrieval, stealth, or success probability.
- Do not make Ember's loyalty, affection, spiritual significance or story relationship a purchasable meter.
- Premium accessories must preserve Ember's locked appearance and recognizable silhouette; cosmetic variants require visual-canon review.
- Do not sell random accessories, eggs, crates, spins or chance-based companion rewards.
- Do not imply that buying Ember grants divine favour, spiritual authority, or a special relationship with God.

**Recommended launch approach:** launch one optional Ember cosmetic/interaction pass only after the free Ember implementation is complete and tested. Keep all product IDs unset until the Creator Hub product exists and has been explicitly approved.

## 3. Pricing and launch approach

Do not lock Robux prices before:
- the game is playable and stable;
- target players can understand exactly what they receive;
- the purchase flow and regional pricing display are tested;
- the price is compared with the amount of content and production effort;
- a small, voluntary player feedback exercise is completed.

Use low-friction, accessible cosmetic pricing rather than premium-priced progression. Do not use artificial scarcity or pressure. Display current prices using Roblox MarketplaceService product information rather than hard-coded UI prices where supported.

Start with one cosmetic product only: **Asher Vance — Signature Appearance**. Add companion selection and premium companion convenience products only after every free companion is complete, balanced and tested, and after purchase entitlements, refunds/support handling, persistence and analytics are reliable.

## 4. Roblox platform economics

Roblox Creator Hub currently documents:
- creators generally receive 70% of Robux spent on their own in-experience Game Passes and Developer Products, with Roblox retaining the platform share;
- Game Passes are one-time purchases;
- Developer Products are repeatable purchases and require correct receipt processing;
- subscriptions renew automatically and have recurring benefit obligations;
- Earned Robux is not the same as purchased Robux;
- DevEx is the official route for eligible creators to exchange Earned Robux for real-world currency.

The current published DevEx eligibility includes at least 30,000 Earned Robux, age 13 or older, verified email, account in good standing and other terms. A creator who is a minor and lacks legal capacity to accept the terms in their country must have a parent/legal guardian accept on their behalf. Roblox retains discretion over approval. Never treat a projected Robux balance as cash or promise a payout.

All cash conversion, tax, payment, identity and guardian steps must follow Roblox's current rules and be handled with a parent/legal guardian where required. Do not share passwords, identity documents or payment information in chat or repository files.

## 5. Funding the original Godot BTV game

Roblox revenue does not automatically transfer into the Godot project. The funding path is:

Roblox experience sales and eligible creator earnings
→ Earned Robux balance
→ official DevEx eligibility and review
→ approved payout to the eligible creator
→ documented budget allocation for the Godot project.

Suggested allocation of **net cash actually received**, after applicable taxes, fees and required obligations:
- 60% — original Godot BTV production fund;
- 25% — Roblox edition maintenance, moderation, testing and approved asset production;
- 15% — reserve for unexpected costs and future development.

These percentages are a planning proposal, not an automatic transfer or guaranteed return. Revisit them quarterly based on actual revenue and costs. Keep a simple ledger of gross Robux sales, platform share, Earned Robux, DevEx requests, cash received, taxes/fees, and each approved allocation. A parent/guardian should supervise any real-world financial administration required by law or Roblox terms.

## 6. Revenue scenarios — illustrative only

Do not forecast cash until actual sales, Earned Robux, DevEx eligibility and payout are known.

Use this planning formula for a qualifying 70%-share in-experience product:
- gross Robux sales = item price × completed purchases;
- creator Robux before any other adjustments = gross sales × 0.70;
- cash received = only the amount approved and paid through DevEx, not the Robux balance.

Example arithmetic only: 100 Robux of qualifying gross sales corresponds to 70 Robux creator share under the documented 70% model. This is not 70 real-world currency units.

## 7. Analytics and release gates

Track:
- new players and returning players;
- session length and day-1/day-7 retention;
- tutorial and opening-mission completion;
- crashes, mobile performance and server errors;
- shop views and purchase conversion;
- refund/support issues;
- cosmetic ownership and entitlement persistence.

Do not optimise only for spending. Prioritise player satisfaction, retention, stability, fair play and age-appropriate design.

Release gates:
1. A complete free core loop.
2. Stable mobile experience and multiplayer testing.
3. Clear shop disclosures and working purchase grant logic.
4. Server-side entitlement verification.
5. Creator/guardian review of financial and platform requirements.
6. Creator approval before any product is published or price is set.

## 8. Implementation notes

- Use Game Passes for permanent one-time cosmetic ownership.
- Use Developer Products only for repeatable defined purchases; grant through ProcessReceipt with idempotent receipt handling.
- Verify ownership server-side; never trust a client-side "purchase succeeded" message.
- Keep product IDs in server-owned configuration and leave them unset until products exist.
- No paid product or monetization code is included in Foundation v0.1.
- Companion-specific premium convenience is a proposal, not approved implementation; all core companion abilities remain available without payment.
- No product creation, public listing, Robux pricing or publication is authorized by this plan alone.
