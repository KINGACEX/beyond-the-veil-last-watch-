# BTV Roblox — Signature Character Presentation v0.1

**Status:** Proposed visual-production brief; not canon-locked and not an approval to create paid products.  
**Scope:** Roblox edition only. All implementation remains under `roblox/`.  
**Subjects:** Asher Vance and Ember.  
**Visual authority:** BTV Foundation #12 Visual/UI/HUD Bible, Character Bible, Ember Companion Bible, and approved reference images.  
**Goal:** Make Asher and Ember feel like flagship, exceptionally polished characters without selling gameplay superiority.

## 1. Product rule

Asher and Ember are central to the free core experience. Their identities, story roles, ordinary interactions and required gameplay functionality are never paywalled.

The premium distinction is **craftsmanship and presentation**, not stronger outcomes:
- richer approved model detail and material quality;
- bespoke, well-polished animation sets;
- memorable but restrained introduction and bonding presentation;
- optional outfits, accessories, poses and profile presentation;
- premium showroom previews with exact contents shown.

The free versions must still look complete, animate naturally and feel cared for. Do not intentionally make the free models ugly, stiff, incomplete or frustrating to make a purchase more attractive.

## 2. Asher Vance — Signature presentation

### Canon and identity locks
- Asher Vance remains the sole playable protagonist in this Roblox adaptation.
- The Roblox adaptation records Asher's starting age as **18**, Agent/Specialist rank and Watcher branch.
- Asher remains human, fallible and accountable. Premium presentation must not portray him as divine, infallible, spiritually superior or above Christ.
- Preserve the approved reference identity: apparent age, face, hairstyle, facial proportions, body proportions, overall appearance and Covenant identity.
- The exact face, hairstyle and detailed appearance must be taken from approved reference assets. Do not invent replacements from this brief.

### Signature visual target
- Grounded cinematic realism; believable young field-operative proportions.
- High-quality skin, hair, cloth, footwear, equipment and material response.
- A premium, tailored Covenant field outfit that respects the visual bible: white base, restrained gold, cross-centred Christian insignia and practical equipment.
- Clear fabric layering, stitching, seams, fasteners, believable wear and carefully fitted equipment.
- Lighting and material detail should read well on a phone without excessive bloom, metallic glare or visual noise.

### Signature animation set
- Distinctive but believable idle and breathing.
- Clean walk/run transitions, starts, stops and turns.
- Contextual interaction, report-reading and equipment-check animations.
- Calm, natural conversational gestures and facial presentation where supported.
- Cinematic introduction and photo poses that do not interrupt normal play.

Animation must not increase movement speed, attack speed, dodge distance, stamina, damage, defence or interaction priority.

### Optional premium wardrobe
- Signature Covenant field outfit variant.
- A coordinated formal/community outfit only if the story context supports it.
- Additional colour/material variants within the established Covenant identity.
- Cosmetic equipment presentation variants that do not change hitboxes, protection or inventory value.

Asher himself, his story, core outfit access and gameplay role remain available to every player. Only optional wardrobe and presentation variants may be premium.

## 3. Ember — Signature companion presentation

### Canon and identity locks
- Ember is a rare, naturally occurring original canid lineage and a creature of God's creation.
- He is an animal companion and field partner, not a weapon, divine being, human speaker or omniscient guide.
- Preserve natural canid anatomy. No fantasy wings, demonic anatomy, cybernetic body, angelic transformation or human speech.
- His exact appearance remains open. The approved Ember reference and Foundation #11 must be reviewed before final model production.
- His limited Veil sensitivity is expressed through fallible behaviour and perception, never through premium-only information.

### Signature visual target
- A highly detailed, species-distinct original canid model with believable anatomy and expressive natural movement.
- Carefully groomed coat/fur materials, subtle colour variation, clear eyes, readable muzzle and natural paw contact.
- High-quality head, ear, tail and shoulder movement; subtle breathing and weight shifts.
- A premium collar/harness and optional accessories designed to fit his established identity.
- Restrained material accents only. No persistent glowing runes, supernatural armour or effects that imply a new power.
- Face and silhouette must remain recognisable at ordinary mobile camera distance.

### Signature animation and bonding set
- Bespoke idle/breathing, alert, walk, faster gait, turn, settle, rest and regroup transitions.
- Distinctive command acknowledgement through posture, ears, head and tail.
- Natural approach, sit/lie-down, brief play and calm bonding interactions in appropriate safe contexts.
- A short, authored introduction/bonding scene and optional photo poses.
- Context-sensitive reactions must respect Ember's survival and protection priorities. He may hesitate, interrupt or refuse an unsafe command when the approved AI system supports it.

Animation polish must not make a command execute faster or improve its result. Premium presentation cannot improve tracking accuracy, Veil sensitivity, detection range, protection, damage, health, speed, retrieval success or mission outcomes.

## 4. Presentation tiers

| Tier | Availability | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Free | Complete Asher and Ember identities, readable models, essential animations, required interactions and full story/gameplay access |
| Signature | Proposed optional cosmetic | Premium outfit/accessory variants, bespoke presentation animations, profile styling and safe-area poses |
| Legacy Collection | Proposed optional bundle | A curated set of Signature cosmetics for Asher and Ember, plus a non-exclusive showcase scene |

These are content concepts only. No Robux prices, product IDs, passes, entitlement code or purchase prompts are authorised by this document.

Do not use randomised paid rewards, countdowns, fake scarcity, expiring cosmetics or pressure prompts. If an item is offered, disclose exactly what it contains and whether it is permanent.

## 5. Covenant Showcase

Proposed safe-area display where every player can:
- inspect Asher and Ember at close range;
- rotate the preview and view approved animation samples;
- compare Core and Signature appearances;
- see exact item contents and ownership state;
- preview free alternatives without purchasing;
- close the display without repeated prompts.

The Showcase is not a mission gate, progression gate or required interaction. No purchase UI is to be implemented until separately approved.

## 6. Production pipeline and visual quality gates

1. Locate and verify the approved Asher and Ember reference assets.
2. Create a proportion/silhouette sheet without changing established identities.
3. Produce the free Core models to the full minimum visual quality bar.
4. Rig and animate the Core models; verify deformation and natural movement.
5. Create Signature cosmetic variants using the same approved identity.
6. Review close, normal gameplay and distant mobile-camera views.
7. Profile memory, rendering cost, shadows and animation with several players present.
8. Import into Roblox Studio and run single-player, multi-client and low-end Android checks.
9. Complete canon, accessibility and free/premium parity review before any release decision.

### Ember model acceptance
- Recognisable natural canid silhouette and approved reference continuity.
- No disallowed fantasy or artificial anatomy.
- Clean deformation through idle, walk, turn, rest and acknowledgement.
- Natural follow spacing and no player/objective obstruction.
- Face, coat and accessories remain readable at mobile distance.
- No hidden information or gameplay advantage is conveyed by visual effects.

### Asher model acceptance
- Recognisable approved face, apparent age, hairstyle and proportions.
- Reads as the same young field-operative across gameplay and cinematics.
- Covenant clothing respects the established visual language.
- Equipment does not imply unapproved armour or combat advantages.
- Animation remains natural and does not change gameplay timings.

### Cross-platform and fairness acceptance
- Core models are not deliberately degraded to sell Signature cosmetics.
- Same command and context produce the same gameplay result for all players.
- No premium-only story, evidence, objective, route or spiritual content.
- Touch UI remains fully usable without premium.
- Multi-client and Android tests pass before claiming runtime readiness.
