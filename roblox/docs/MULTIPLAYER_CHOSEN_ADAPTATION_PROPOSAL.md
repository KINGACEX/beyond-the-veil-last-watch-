# BTV Roblox Multiplayer Chosen Adaptation

**Status:** Creator-requested direction; Roblox-edition proposal.  
**Scope:** Roblox edition only. Does not rewrite the original Godot storyline or source-of-truth canon.  
**Decision requested:** Adopt a Roblox-specific multiplayer continuity in which each player creates/uses their own Chosen operative identity.

## 1. Requested direction

The creator requested:
- every player should be a Chosen;
- Asher Vance should be purchasable as a Robux-funded character skin;
- the Roblox edition should support multiplayer.

## 2. Safe continuity interpretation

For the Roblox multiplayer continuity, each player controls their own individual Chosen operative. This is a Roblox-specific adaptation and should not be interpreted as changing the original single-player narrative, where Asher Vance remains the established protagonist.

"Chosen" must remain a narrative calling/role, not a measurable spiritual rank, holiness score, purchasable status, divine-favour meter, or competitive leaderboard category. Players do not purchase being Chosen. All eligible players receive the same narrative status through play; no paid product grants it.

The story must continue to place God as ultimate authority and Christ above the Covenant and every player character. Every player character remains human and fallible. Do not present players as divine, equal to Christ, or spiritually superior to other people.

## 3. Asher Vance premium appearance

Offer an optional, one-time cosmetic entitlement called **Asher Vance — Signature Appearance**.

Rules:
- It changes the player's visual presentation only.
- It does not replace the player's saved identity or rewrite story events.
- It grants no damage, health, speed, access, mission advantage, currency multiplier, special authority or exclusive spiritual ability.
- It must not imply that the purchaser is literally the original Asher in the core Godot continuity.
- It must use the approved Asher character reference and visual consistency lock.
- Avoid using the name "skin" in narrative dialogue; use "appearance" or "character look" in the shop.
- The purchase must be clearly described as optional and cosmetic.

If the existing story requires Asher as an NPC/protagonist in a scene, the premium appearance must not create duplicate-Asher narrative contradictions. Use an explicit multiplayer presentation convention (e.g. player-avatar appearance layer) and document it before scene production.

## 4. Continuity boundaries

- Original Godot continuity remains unchanged.
- Existing BTV source-of-truth canon remains higher authority.
- This document is a Roblox adaptation proposal and implementation boundary.
- Any conflict with established story sequence, Asher's role, Covenant hierarchy, Ember, Church, Communion or theology must be escalated rather than improvised.
- The original opening sequence remains Communion context → Covenant alert → Scout Report/evidence review → Ridgeview journey → investigation/response → final report.

## 5. Approval and implementation gates

Before calling this adaptation locked:
1. Creator confirms that "every player is a Chosen" applies only to the Roblox multiplayer continuity.
2. Creator confirms whether Asher's appearance is a cosmetic avatar overlay or a full character selection presentation. Recommended: cosmetic avatar presentation only.
3. Character appearance and story-scene duplication rules are reviewed.
4. No Godot canon document is edited as part of this Roblox-only change.
