# BTV Roblox Multiplayer Chosen Adaptation

**Status: SUPERSEDED.** Historical proposal retained for traceability; not an active design or implementation requirement.

## Superseding decision

- Asher Vance remains the sole Chosen and central protagonist.
- Other players are their own individual Covenant operatives, not additional Chosen and not duplicate Ashers.
- Multiplayer missions are cooperative Covenant operations alongside the established Asher storyline.
- The original Godot continuity and source-of-truth canon remain unchanged.
- No player purchases Chosen status, Covenant rank, spiritual authority or story importance.
- An Asher Signature Appearance is not an approved player identity system. Any future cosmetic presentation requires a separate review and must not create duplicate-Asher scenes or alter player identity.

## Why this proposal is superseded

The former direction that every player should be a Chosen conflicts with the established sole-Chosen continuity. Its proposed purchasable Asher character presentation also creates identity and duplicate-character risks. The established creator decision resolves the conflict: Asher remains the sole Chosen; multiplayer users are Covenant operatives.
