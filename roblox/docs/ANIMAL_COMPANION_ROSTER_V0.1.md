# BTV Roblox Animal Companion Roster v0.1

**Status:** Creator-selected roster; detailed animal designs are proposed for review, not canon-locked.
**Applies to:** BTV Roblox edition only. Keep the original Godot BTV continuity separate.
**Selected roster:** Ember, Eagle, Fox, Bear, Owl, Leopard, Horse, Falcon.
**Acquisition direction:** Some premium animals, with free alternatives.
**Design rule:** Animal identity changes presentation and playstyle emphasis, never access to essential evidence, mission completion, or fair competitive outcomes.

## 1. Design pillars

1. Every companion must be useful, responsive, readable and enjoyable without spending Robux.
2. Each animal has a recognisable silhouette, movement language, temperament and support role.
3. All mission-critical functions have a free route. A premium animal may be an alternate expression of a role already represented by a free animal.
4. Species abilities are bounded support behaviours, not supernatural powers. No unexplained Veil abilities or new metaphysics are established here.
5. Companions are animals and field partners, not weapons, disposable tools or substitutes for player judgement.
6. Preserve Ember's established visual identity, role and approved commands. Do not redesign Ember to fit this roster.
7. The roster does not establish animal species, origin or lore in the original Godot continuity.
8. No companion purchase grants spiritual status, divine favour, prayer outcomes, Scripture authority or a theological advantage.

## 3. Individual companion design cards

### 3.1 Ember — versatile field partner

- **Role:** The familiar, all-round field companion and reference implementation for the system.
- **Personality:** Loyal, alert, intelligent, steady under pressure; communicates through natural body language and clear command acknowledgement.
- **Visual direction:** Preserve the established Ember reference exactly as the source of truth. Improve fur/material quality, facial readability, animation quality and mobile rendering without changing recognisable features, proportions or role.
- **Free functional kit:** Preserve the seven approved commands: Attack, Protect, Scout, Track, Retrieve, Hold, Regroup. Their detailed behaviour remains subject to the existing command design and mission safety constraints.
- **Distinctive interaction:** Clear, expressive acknowledgement and reliable follow/recall behaviour.
- **Premium options:** Optional approved accessory collection, expanded non-combat bonding animations, profile presentation, alternate idle/rest animation set and configurable command-wheel layout.
- **Restrictions:** No paid-only command, stronger combat performance, superior tracking accuracy, exclusive clue detection, or paid affection/loyalty.

### 3.2 Eagle — distant observation

- **Role:** Aerial environmental observation and broad scene awareness.
- **Personality:** Composed, watchful, independent; responds promptly but does not behave like a controllable drone.
- **Visual direction:** Large, readable wing silhouette; realistic feather layering; grounded flight arcs, natural landing poses and restrained animation.
- **Free-equivalent role:** Owl provides the free observation alternative.
- **Functional boundary:** May provide a broad visual observation from a valid, visible vantage point. It cannot reveal hidden evidence, mark enemies through walls, expose secret objectives, or bypass investigation.
- **Premium value:** Species identity, animation/presentation sets, profile card and optional safe-context observation UI shortcuts only.
- **Restrictions:** No greater detection radius, secret highlighting, map reveal or mission information unavailable through the free Owl route.

### 3.3 Fox — agile explorer

- **Role:** Agile ground companion for exploration and nearby environmental interaction.
- **Personality:** Curious, quick, cautious and expressive; investigates ordinary environmental points without forcing the player into an interaction.
- **Visual direction:** Compact silhouette, detailed coat, expressive ears and tail, quick but natural footwork; avoid exaggerated cartoon proportions.
- **Free-equivalent role:** Fox is itself a free role option; it also provides a free alternative to the premium Leopard's agility emphasis.
- **Functional boundary:** May accompany the player through suitable terrain and react to ordinary points of interest. It does not discover hidden mission evidence or bypass locked routes.
- **Premium value:** Optional animation sets, cosmetic accessories, profile presentation and safe-context routine presets.
- **Restrictions:** No speed advantage that changes race/mission balance, no exclusive traversal routes and no paid clue-finding.

### 3.4 Bear — protective presence

- **Role:** Defensive positioning, steady escort presence and formation support.
- **Personality:** Calm, patient, dependable and deliberate; avoids needless agitation.
- **Visual direction:** Realistic anatomy, grounded weight, restrained movement, readable stance changes and believable fur/material response.
- **Free-equivalent role:** Ember's existing free Protect role and formation support.
- **Functional boundary:** Supports defensive positioning and companion formation within the game's shared balance rules. It is not a damage or health upgrade.
- **Premium value:** Bear species ownership, approved cosmetic presentation, non-combat rest/bonding animations and profile themes.
- **Restrictions:** No extra health, damage, armour, knockback, crowd control, threat generation or mission success advantage.

### 3.5 Owl — quiet observer

- **Role:** Observation, ambient awareness and calm environmental presence.
- **Personality:** Patient, observant, deliberate and quiet.
- **Visual direction:** Detailed feather structure, clear eye expression, natural head movement and believable perching/short-flight transitions.
- **Access:** Proposed free.
- **Functional boundary:** Provides the same broad observation category as Eagle and Falcon, with its own visual and interaction style. No hidden evidence or secret-object detection.
- **Premium value:** Optional feather-safe cosmetic variations only where visual canon permits, animations, audio presentation and profile styling.
- **Restrictions:** No paid-exclusive vision mode, night vision, wall penetration, objective highlighting or additional tactical information.

### 3.6 Leopard — agile traversal specialist

- **Role:** Agile movement companion and route-following support.
- **Personality:** Focused, reserved, confident and attentive to the player.
- **Visual direction:** Distinctive spotted coat, athletic but natural anatomy, controlled movement and readable silhouette. Avoid exaggerated predatory presentation.
- **Free-equivalent role:** Fox provides free agile exploration support.
- **Functional boundary:** Follows suitable paths and supports ordinary exploration. It does not unlock shortcuts, climb otherwise inaccessible mission routes or grant a player movement buff.
- **Premium value:** Leopard species identity, non-combat animation sets, cosmetic presentation and safe-context quick-action layouts.
- **Restrictions:** No increased player speed, stealth, damage, detection or access to otherwise inaccessible areas.

### 3.7 Horse — travel companion

- **Role:** Travel, long-distance exploration and possible mounted travel in explicitly designed safe/open-world contexts.
- **Personality:** Steady, social, dependable and responsive to calm handling.
- **Visual direction:** Realistic equine proportions, convincing gait transitions, responsive ears/head, hoof contact and tack that fits the world.
- **Access:** Proposed free.
- **Functional boundary:** Mounted travel is a separate feature requiring locomotion, camera, collision, dismount, accessibility and multiplayer testing. If mounted travel is not ready, launch the horse as a follow/travel companion without implying that mounting is available.
- **Premium value:** Optional tack/cosmetics, stable presentation, idle/rest animations and profile customisation.
- **Restrictions:** No paid-only travel routes, speed advantage in competitive contexts, stamina advantage, mission skip or exclusive map access.

### 3.8 Falcon — responsive aerial companion

- **Role:** Aerial observation with a quicker, more responsive presentation style than the Owl.
- **Personality:** Alert, energetic, disciplined and responsive.
- **Visual direction:** Compact aerodynamic silhouette, detailed feather structure, believable flight acceleration and clean perch/return animation.
- **Free-equivalent role:** Owl provides the free observation alternative.
- **Functional boundary:** A different presentation and interaction style for the same bounded observation category. Cannot reveal hidden clues, expose enemies through cover or bypass investigation.
- **Premium value:** Falcon species identity, animation/audio presentation, profile card and command-interface personalisation.
- **Restrictions:** No superior detection, longer reveal range, faster mission completion or exclusive reconnaissance information.

## 4. Free and premium access model

### Proposed free roster
- Ember — versatile field partner.
- Fox — agile exploration.
- Owl — observation.
- Horse — travel companion, subject to mounted-travel readiness.

### Proposed premium candidates
- Eagle — premium alternative in the observation category; Owl remains the free alternative.
- Bear — premium alternative in the protective category; Ember retains the free protective role.
- Leopard — premium alternative in the agile category; Fox remains the free alternative.
- Falcon — premium alternative in the observation category; Owl remains the free alternative.

This is a **species/access distinction**, not a power tier. Premium candidates must be balanced to the same functional ceiling as their free counterparts. A premium species may feel different through animation, silhouette, temperament and interaction style, but it cannot produce a better mission result.

## 5. Universal companion system requirements

Every animal must support the shared quality baseline where biologically and mechanically appropriate:

- Natural follow and formation positioning.
- Hold/stay and regroup/recall.
- Safe recovery if stuck.
- Clear command acknowledgement and visible current state.
- Interruptible behaviour and context validation.
- Reliable persistence of selected companion and earned ownership.
- Touch-first mobile controls with large targets.
- Multiplayer replication that avoids jitter, teleport abuse and conflicting ownership.
- Appropriate idle, rest, follow, recall and transition animations.
- Respectful animal behaviour; no needless distress, cruelty or disposable-pet framing.

Species-specific movement is allowed, but the player-facing command language and interaction reliability should remain consistent.

## 6. Premium fairness and monetisation guardrails

Premium may provide:
- Access to an optional premium animal species.
- Cosmetic accessories and approved appearance variants.
- Alternative non-combat animations and audio presentation.
- Companion profile cards, showcase presentation and safe-area interactions.
- Configurable command layouts, saved safe-context presets and contextual shortcuts.

Premium must not provide:
- More health, damage, armour, speed, stealth, attack frequency or resistance.
- Better tracking accuracy, detection radius, hidden-object discovery or mission information.
- Exclusive mission routes, objectives, evidence, story content or completion options.
- Faster cooldowns or stronger command results.
- Paid affection, loyalty, spiritual status or relationship outcomes.
- Randomised paid animal eggs, crates, spins or loot boxes.

A free animal must remain a complete alternative for every gameplay role represented by a premium animal. The free alternative must not be deliberately slower, less reliable or less enjoyable.

## 7. Visual production standards

Use the established BTV realistic/cinematic direction:
- Realistic anatomy and species-appropriate proportions.
- High-quality fur, feather, skin and tack materials with mobile-friendly shader budgets.
- Distinct silhouettes readable at phone-screen scale.
- Natural locomotion and grounded foot/wing contact.
- Consistent lighting and material response across the roster.
- LODs, animation budgets and texture limits designed for low-end Android devices.
- No visual effects that imply supernatural abilities without explicit canon approval.

**Ember is the visual consistency anchor.** Its source reference must be obtained from the approved BTV visual assets before producing a replacement model. Other animals may establish a coherent visual family but must not cause Ember's identity to drift.

## 8. Implementation order and release gates

### Phase A — foundation
1. Confirm source visual references and companion scale standards.
2. Build one reusable companion controller and state model.
3. Implement follow, hold, regroup, safe recall, status and persistence.
4. Test touch controls and multiplayer replication.
5. Validate Ember without changing its canon design.

### Phase B — free role coverage
1. Add Fox and Owl as free role representatives.
2. Add Horse only when travel behaviour is stable; otherwise defer mounted travel.
3. Verify that every opening-slice mission can be completed with the free roster.
4. Run mobile performance and accessibility checks.

### Phase C — premium candidates
1. Prototype Eagle, Bear, Leopard and Falcon as alternate species.
2. Run role-equivalence tests against Owl, Ember and Fox.
3. Add only cosmetic/presentation premium products after free alternatives pass.
4. Do not create product IDs, prices or Creator Hub listings without separate creator approval.

### Phase D — scale
Expand the roster only after the shared controller, animation pipeline, persistence and multiplayer performance are stable.

## 9. Acceptance tests

- A player who spends zero Robux can complete every mission and access all essential evidence.
- Owl offers the same essential observation category as Eagle and Falcon.
- Ember's free protective role remains a valid alternative to Bear.
- Fox remains a valid free alternative to Leopard.
- Premium species do not reveal additional evidence or alter mission outcomes.
- Equivalent support commands produce equivalent functional results in equivalent contexts.
- Companion switching and ownership are server-validated and persist correctly.
- All animals remain responsive and recoverable on low-end Android devices.
- Other players see coherent companion behaviour in multiplayer.
- Horse mounting, if implemented, passes camera, collision, dismount, accessibility and multiplayer tests before release.
- Ember's established appearance and role remain unchanged.
- No new Veil lore, supernatural animal ability or Godot continuity change is introduced by this Roblox roster.
